# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## What worked
- Sleeping in a bed beside the group worked on some nights (Days 164, 194, 205, 220; white bed at -270,67,336 on days 230 to 235 and night 250; yellow bed at -263,67,353 on nights 251 and 258). Sleep worked once I walked back beside the group after a refusal (days 250, 251, 258). Refusals were common; the bed name does not decide it.
- Killing a cow, pig or sheep gives meat: beef from cow (Day 205 at -391,64,259; Day 261 at -288,67,257 west of the lake), porkchop from pig (Day 205 at -353,66,236; Day 210 at -392,72,222), mutton from sheep (Day 210 near -276,66,215).
- Walking with goto can stop short or land in water. Unstick, then walk again in short steps along dry grass (Days 205, 210, 261).
- Handing food works only when the person is in reach and in sight (porkchop to Picard, Day 210; wheat to Picard, Day 262). Out of reach, the give fails and the food stays with me.
- Crafting at a table works: 4 oak planks from 1 log, sticks from planks, then a wooden sword (Days 205, 220, 256, table near -251,67,356).
- Pooling a search with the group found food on days 221 to 236. Solo or pooled searches on days 247 to 264 found almost nothing except the one west cow on day 261.
