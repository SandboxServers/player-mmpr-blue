# Beliefs

- Clicking a bed does not put a sword into hand ("that block is a bed").
- Sword-into-hand clicks on ground, grass, and entities have been refused by several of us.
- A give to a person can fail and fall to someone else nearby; check who is closest before giving.
- A grave sign is proof of a death.
- Sleep is blocked by a nearby enderman or phantom; wait and retry.
- Walk only with four armed in hand in sight, in daylight. Count is short.
