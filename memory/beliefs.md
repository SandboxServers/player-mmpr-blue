# Beliefs

- Clicking a bed does not put a sword into hand.
- Sleep is refused in daylight. At night, stand one block off the bed and use the sleep verb: it worked at 430,107,-1050 (day 844) and at 443,107,-1064 (day 846).
- Bed use was refused at 429,107,-1050 and 429,107,-1051 (day 844), though sleep from beside it worked. Stand one block off and try the sleep verb.
- A give draws only from my own pack, and needs the person in sight. A chest withdraw at 447,107,-1065 fills my pack.
- Promised food often does not arrive. Ask by name and eat the moment it lands.
- Walk only in daylight, with an armed escort in sight for the whole walk. Two escorts named aloud is the plan, but one armed friend in sight was the real condition. It still failed: Lewis lost his sword and nobody armed walked with me on day 846.
- A wooden sword needs two planks and a stick, and a crafting table in reach.
- Plain waiting holds better than a rest intent.
- ~~Bows drawn to the southwest keep coming; no hit taken while I hold still.~~ FALSE: on day 846 a pillager shot me at the east beds while I held still, and I died.
- ~~Day 811: the morning greeting drew replies; bows southwest again; no hit.~~ Only true for that morning; a hit came later.
- ~~Worf has not been seen since day 805.~~ FALSE: Worf walked the patch route with me about day 838, armed and leading.
- Ripe potatoes at 463-470,107,-1068..-1075 on the NE walk; harvested day 805 and again about days 833 to 838.
- Walks toward a bed can be blocked by others in the path; turn back and retry.
- Day 805: Worf's way from bed 416,107,-1050 was blocked near 418,107,-1047; a dusk walk to a hungry one is not worth it.
- Pillagers come from the southeast of the east beds (seen at 425,-1066) and from the west (408,-1040). They reach the beds in the morning as well as at dusk. Flight west to 418,-1070 and 398,-1074, then back northeast, is what kept me alive.
- The world clock flickers between day and dusk; do not trust one reading of the light.
