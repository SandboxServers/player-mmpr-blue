# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## What worked
- Sleeping in a bed at night worked on some nights (Days 164, 194, 205, 220; white bed at -270,67,336 on days 230 to 235 and night 250; yellow bed at -262,67,353 on night 251). Sleep with the group in sight. Creepers, phantoms and skeletons refuse sleep or attack near the beds at night. Sleep worked once I walked back beside the group after a refusal (days 250 and 251).
- Pooling a search with the group found food on earlier days (221 to 236). Held, but weakened: the pooled searches of days 247 to 253 found nothing.
- Killing a cow, pig or sheep gives meat: beef from cow (Day 205, cow at -391,64,259 gave 2 beef), porkchop from pig (Day 205 at -353,66,236 gave 2; Day 210 pig at -392,72,222 gave 3), mutton from sheep (Day 210 near -276,66,215 gave 2).
- Walking to a spot with goto can stop one block short, fail, or put me in water. Unstick, then walk again in short steps along dry grass (worked Days 205 and 210).
- Handing food to someone works when they are in reach. Porkchop to Picard worked (Day 210). Giving to someone out of reach fails and the food stays with me; check reach first. Days 235 to 236: mutton give to Jason and Tommy failed several times while they were out of sight; I kept the mutton until they came into view.
- Crafting works at a table: 4 oak planks from 1 log, sticks from planks, then a wooden sword (Day 205 and Day 220, table near -251,67,356). Planks from logs also worked for a bridge or wall.

## What did not work
- ~~Searching for food at the farm and sheep spots finds nothing.~~ STRUCK OUT: sheep and pigs were found on the grass near -276,66,212 and -392,72,222. The farm spots near the beds still gave little.
- ~~Relying on one cow sighting.~~ A "cow" at -285,65,235 was a donkey (Day 205 and Day 220 search), and the cow at -262,67,244 was gone.

- Travel to a far point stopped at the water edge several times. Use short steps along dry ground.
- Going unarmed in the open: with no sword I could not fight a skeleton or a drowned (Day 205, Day 220). I have had no sword since about day 245.
- ~~"Dirt is plentiful, about 30."~~ STRUCK OUT: dirt was gone from my pack by Day 205. Do not count on dirt.
- ~~"Salmon at the lake is a food source."~~ STRUCK OUT: unproven; I never caught fish, and I drowned in the lake each time I went near it.
- Waiting alone on the grass for a group that does not come is slow. Go to the group's spot, or let the group come to you.
- Days 221 to 236: the pig spot at -168,89,225 was empty when we arrived; the west grass search and the south grass search found no animals. A wide search is not a plan; a known spot is.
- Days 247 to 253: the west grass (about -300 to -335 east-west, 317 to 330 north-south, and 229 to 258 further south) held no animals on at least five trips. Do not walk it again without a sighting.
- Salmon in the deep water near -305,61,232 and the bank at -325,64,240: I nearly drowned again (day 233, air down to 8). Do not go into the lake for fish.
- Sleep at the yellow beds near -271,67,318 to -271,67,326 failed on many nights (days 221 to 253); phantoms attacked at dusk near the west grass edge (day 230 and day 235), and one attack killed me. Do not stay out on the grass after dusk.
- ~~"Sleep only works at night, so I'll scout."~~ Still believed, but not proven: sleep failed in the day too, and I never confirmed the cause.
