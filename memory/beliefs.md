## Why things failed
- Named walks failed because the group did not come into sight, and I waited too long in the open.
- Holding food for someone who is "near" by report, but not in sight, meant the food stayed in my pack and I starved closer to death.
- Night in the open is death: phantoms come from above and hit from any side. Pillagers shoot at dusk and again in the early morning, and I died to them on nights 912, 935 and 964.
- I was unarmed for the whole stretch from day 906 on, so I could not answer anything that attacked me.
