# Beliefs

## What worked
- Feeding by hand works only when the person is in my sight and reachable. A give that I sent with someone out of sight did not land (Beverly, Zack, Worf, Bcrusher, days 902-906).
- Walking in daylight with an armed friend in sight was the safer way; named walks were often called off when the group did not gather in sight.
- Beds at night: the bed at 443,107,-1064/-1065 and the white bed at 443,107,-1037/-1036 let me sleep and wake (day 886-891 by my summary). Beds at 415,-1037 and 413,-1041 let me sleep once in the night before the refusals began.
- Eating the moment food lands keeps hunger from falling further.

## What did not work (struck out where proven false)

- ~~Bare hands do nothing to phantoms.~~ Proven too weak: fighting a phantom with my hands dropped me from 11 to 3 health (day 906). Bare hands did not save me.

## Why things failed
- Named walks failed because the group did not come into sight, and I waited too long in the open.
- Holding food for someone who is "near" by report, but not in sight, meant the food stayed in my pack and I starved closer to death.
- Night in the open is death: phantoms come from above and hit from any side. Pillagers come in the morning too.

## Still open

- Who among the row will hold a sword and walk with me.

## Dangers
- Pillagers: east beds from the southeast (425,-1066), and the west (408,-1040). They reach the beds in morning as well as dusk.
- Phantoms: at night over open ground, north and west of the row (day 906).
- The clock flickers between day and dusk; do not trust one reading of the light.
