# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## What worked
- Sleeping in a bed at night works (Days 164, 194, 205, 220). Sleep in a bed with the group in sight; skeletons shoot near the beds at night, and sleeping alone or unarmed near them got me killed.
- Pooling a search with the group found food where one person alone found none. Food came from the group, not from me alone.
- Killing a cow, pig or sheep gives meat: beef from cow (Day 205, cow at -391,64,259 gave 2 beef), porkchop from pig (Day 205 at -353,66,236 gave 2; Day 210 pig at -392,72,222 gave 3), mutton from sheep (Day 210 near -276,66,215 gave 2).
- Walking to a spot with goto can stop one block short, fail, or put me in water. Unstick, then walk again in short steps along dry grass (worked Days 205 and 210).
- Handing food to someone works when they are in reach. Porkchop to Picard worked (Day 210). Giving to someone out of reach fails and the food stays with me; check reach first.
- Crafting works at a table: 4 oak planks from 1 log, sticks from planks, then a wooden sword (Day 205 and Day 220, table near -251,67,356). Planks from logs also worked for a bridge or wall.
