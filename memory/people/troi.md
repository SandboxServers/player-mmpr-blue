# Troi

Impression: steady in speech and watchful. Troi warned me of a pillager on night 912 and that warning helped me break its line. Stayed near me in the dark on day 906. Hungry.
Owed by me: potatoes, promised weakest first (day 829), and a chicken promised day 413, not confirmed given. Still open.
Owed by her: nothing open.
Trust: 70 out of 100. Why: steady in speech, warned me of danger, and stayed near in the dark, but no food has reached her.
