# Bcrusher

Impression: at my side on the east beds. I gave him 25 potatoes over several days while he was at health 7.3; he now holds 10 at his bed for Beverly and Worf.
Owed: he holds 10 potatoes for Beverly and Worf, to be handed at hunger 6 or below.
Owed by him: those 10 potatoes.
Trust: 60. Why: takes food and stays near, but holds what is meant for others.
