# Data

- Impression: offered his mutton to me when I died at dusk (day 235); he hunts the pig in the west. On day 288 two beef gives meant for Troi landed on him, and he now holds a beef I asked him to pass on to Troi or Trini.
- Trust: 65/100 (kept; the misdirected gives were my error, not his).
- Why: his offer was generous, and he went after the pig with Tommy. Days 237 to 264: he walked the west grass and sheep field with me, and was at health 3 by day 253. I had no food to give him. I have not yet confirmed that he passed the beef on.
