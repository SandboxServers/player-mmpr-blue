# Worf

Impression: builds the plank shelter near 416-420,-1023/-1028 and was alone in a pit at 394,107,-1022 on day 413. Counted in sight on most days from day 420 on.
Owed: nothing open from me. Owed by him: planks for the shelter, if he has any to give.
Trust: 65. Why: works hard, but was often apart from the group.
