# Lewis

Impression: walked to the row when called, and came with me to the sword spot in daylight on day 844. He died to arrows on day 912 at 393,-1047, and I have not seen him since.
Owed: a named walk to the potato patch with his sword in hand, not yet made.
Owed by him: nothing open.
Trust: 60 out of 100. Why: present and steady, but he has no sword now and is gone.
