# Picard

- Impression: keeps getting stranded in the water near the bridge; needs help and guidance to keep to the grass. Calm at the beds.
- Trust: 60/100 (kept).
- Day 268: I found him stranded on the north shore near the salmon and told him where the animals were. No food has passed between us since the wheat on day 262.
- Day 262: I gave him my one wheat for the bread pool, and he took it while in reach. A small step: he is on the grass and at the beds.
- Why: I gave him 3 porkchop (Day 210) and he ate. He stayed in the water too long, which cost food and risk. Days 221 to 236 he was with the group on the grass and walked the pig road with us; I have not given him food since. Night 251 he slept beside me in the yellow bed. I want him on the grass and in reach before I hand over food.
