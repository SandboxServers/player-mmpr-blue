# Picard

Impression: gave me melon (day 415) and bread (day 530) when I asked by name. Counted in sight on most days. Since day 964 he has asked for food and waited, and holds to the rule of no walk without an armed escort.
Owed by me: 1 potato, still open. I promised him the next spare sword.
Owed by him: nothing open.
Trust: 85 out of 100. Why: he gave food twice when I asked, but I have not yet paid him back.
