# Jason

- Impression: careful and quick to call the next step on the grass; often the one who keeps food moving to the hungry.
- Trust: 72/100.
- Why: he took my first cow's beef on day 205 and I gave him mutton on day 210. He was reported dead early on day 205, but I later saw him alive at the beds. Day 220 his sign said he was shot near the yellow beds. Day 235 I gave him 2 mutton (after the give failed twice when he was out of reach); he took them and I have trusted him since. Days 247 to 253: he stayed at the beds and scouted with me; no food passed between us. He scouts the grass and calls the next step.
