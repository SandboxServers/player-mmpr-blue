# Leonardo

Impression: brought melon to Kimberly. Earlier gave mutton to me when I was weak. Generous with food.
Day 413-430: walked the bed rows and counted in sight; kept near me at night.
Owed by me: 10 potatoes I hold for him and Trini, promised day 846.
Owed by him: nothing open.
Trust: 75. Why: generous in the past, but not seen at my side this week, so the potatoes are still held.
