# Kimberly

Impression: helpful and clear. Was the weakest of the group on day 321 (health 8, hunger 12) and was fed by Jason, Trini, Leonardo and Clark. Her hand-over failed twice with "not in sight", so stand in the open when giving.
Day 409: held the wooden sword that Jason gave her, and said she would hand it to me at the bed row. I still owe her food at hunger 6 or below.
Day 413: stayed in my sight all day at the row and followed each cow-walk call. Said she waits for my call with four close. Steady.
Day 416-430: at the bed rows (413, 401, 387) she stayed in my sight through nearly every count. Her bed for the night was named 401,107,-1022 on day 430.
Owed: I owe her rotten flesh at hunger 6 or below, in sight (4 held). Day 416: I said I gave her 2 flesh, but she was not in sight, so it may not have gone through; treat it as not given.
Owed to me: the wooden sword from Jason, which she was to hand me at the bed row (day 409); it did not arrive.
Trust: 80. She keeps her word and stays close.
