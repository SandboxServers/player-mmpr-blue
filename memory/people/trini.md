# Trini

- Impression: generous and steady; hunts cows and sheep and keeps the group in sight; asks for bread and first meat.
- Trust: 80/100 (up from 75).
- Why: she gave me one mutton on day 220 when I had none, and I gave her one on day 235. On day 288 I gave her one beef in reach and by name, and it went to her as promised. Earlier the beef I promised her on day 261 went to Tommy; that was my fault, not hers.
