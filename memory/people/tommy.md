# Tommy

- Impression: steady companion; keeps to the grass, leads the bridge work, and sleeps in the same beds as me.
- Trust: 70/100 (down from 75).
- Why: we walked and slept together on days 205 to 236. He lost everything in the lake (Day 210) and I gave him one mutton, which he accepted. He kept the bridge end and took my porkchop. Days 235 to 236 he went out of sight before I could hand him mutton, and he was hunting the pig near -336,64,229 with Riker. I trust him, but he is hard to reach in a hunt.
