# Tommy

- Impression: steady companion; keeps to the grass, leads the bridge work, and sleeps in the same beds as me.
- Trust: 75/100.
- Why: we walked and slept together on days 205 to 220. He lost everything in the lake (Day 210) and I gave him one mutton, which he accepted. He kept the bridge end and took my porkchop. I have not been let down by him.
