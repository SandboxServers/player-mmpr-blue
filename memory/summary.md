# Summary (as of day 771 sleep, near bed 429,107,-1037)

I am Billy. Health 20, hunger 20 at sleep. Holding nothing: my things lie near 411,107,-1056 where pillagers killed me (day 768).

Beds: my bed 429,107,-1037 (I stand at 430,107,-1037). Also 429,107,-1036, 429,107,-1050, 429,107,-1051.

Row: Zack, Trini, Lewis, Worf, Beverly, Kimberly, Jason and Riker hold at the beds, in sight. No verified route west past x=370. No sheep seen. Cows seen near 339,104,-979. Data seen near them.

Rules: walk only in daylight, named aloud first, with two armed escorts in sight. Sleep at night in a bed, in sight of the row. Feed anyone at hunger 10 or below, weakest first. Do not go west of x=370. Do not go north-west toward the pillagers.

Lessons: using a bed by touch fails; call sleep at the bed to rest. Pillagers shot me near the beds once; stay in the row's sight.

Recent: Day 771 quiet, no walks, no one below 10 hunger reported.

Next: at first light, say good morning, check hunger, feed anyone at 10 or below, ask the row for a verified route west and for sheep.
