Beds: my bed 429,107,-1037 (I stand at 430,107,-1037). Also 429,107,-1036 (Kimberly), 429,107,-1050, 429,107,-1051.

Recent: Day 765 quiet. Everyone holding. Kimberly 15 health, 17 hunger. Worf 14 hunger.

Next: at first light, say good morning, check hunger, feed anyone at 10 or below, ask again for a verified route west and sheep.
