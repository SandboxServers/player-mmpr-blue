I am Billy. Health 20, hunger 20. Holding nothing.
Bed: 429,107,-1037 (mine). Other beds: 429,107,-1036, 429,107,-1064, 429,107,-1065, 443,107,-1064, 443,107,-1065.

Row: Zack, Trini, Lewis, Worf, Beverly, Kimberly, Jason, Riker, Laforge, Chapman (John, at 444, health 11), Picard, Wesley, MacDonald, Clark (William), Crusher, Troi (Deanna). Holding at the beds, in sight.

No verified route west past x=370. No sheep seen. No pillager news. Data last seen near 339,104,-979. Cow sightings near 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113 are unverified (Zack's notes).

Rules: walk only in daylight, named aloud first, with two armed escorts in sight. Sleep at night in a bed in sight of the row. Feed anyone at hunger 10 or below, weakest first. Do not go west of x=370 or north-west toward the pillagers.

Next: at first light, say good morning, check hunger and health, ask the row for food, a verified route, sheep and news. Feed anyone at 10 or below first.
