# Summary (day 791, dusk, at bed 430,107,-1037)

I am Billy. Health 20, hunger 20. Holding nothing.
Bed: 429,107,-1037 (mine). Other beds: 429,107,-1036, 429,107,-1064, 429,107,-1065, 443,107,-1064, 443,107,-1065.
My dropped things lie near 411,107,-1056 where pillagers killed me (day 768). Do not go there.

Row: Zack, Trini, Lewis, Worf, Beverly, Kimberly, Jason, Riker, Laforge, Chapman, Picard, Wesley, MacDonald, Clark, Crusher, Troi. All holding at the beds, in sight.

No verified route west past x=370. No sheep seen. No pillager news. Bows heard far to the south-west. Data last seen near 339,104,-979.
Food: only Troi's one potato. Hunger: Worf 14, MacDonald 16, Trini 17, Troi 17.

Rules: walk only in daylight, named aloud first, with two armed escorts in sight. Sleep at night in a bed in sight of the row. Feed anyone at hunger 10 or below, weakest first. Do not go west of x=370 or north-west toward the pillagers.

Next: at morning, say good morning, check hunger, and ask the row for food, a verified route, sheep and news.
