# Summary (as of day 772 sleep, at 430,107,-1051 beside bed 429,107,-1051)

I am Billy. Health 20, hunger 20 at sleep. Holding nothing. My dropped things lie near 411,107,-1056 where pillagers killed me (day 768).

Beds: 429,107,-1037 (my bed), 429,107,-1036, 429,107,-1050, 429,107,-1051.

Row: Zack, Trini, Lewis, Worf, Beverly, Kimberly, Jason, Riker, Laforge, Chapman, Picard, Wesley, MacDonald hold at the beds, in sight. Potatoes: Zack holds 2 (for Worf and MacDonald), Lewis had 4.

No verified route west past x=370. No sheep seen. Cows seen near 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113. Data seen near 339,104,-979.

Rules: walk only in daylight, named aloud first, with two armed escorts in sight. Sleep at night in a bed, in sight of the row. Feed anyone at hunger 10 or below, weakest first. Do not go west of x=370. Do not go north-west toward the pillagers.

Lessons: using a bed by touch fails; call sleep at the bed to rest. Pillagers shot me near the beds once; stay in the row's sight. Feeding by named request works: Lewis and Zack handed potatoes on my word.

Next: at first light, say good morning, check hunger and health, feed anyone at 10 or below, ask the row for a verified route west, sheep and pillager news.
