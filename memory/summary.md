# Summary (day 811, slept beside bed 443,107,-1023)

I am Billy. Health 20, hunger 20 at sleep. Holding 3 potatoes kept for Worf and a wooden sword.
Beds: 443,107,-1023 (where I slept), 443,107,-1022, 429,107,-1037, 429,107,-1036.
Sleep works at night by standing beside a bed and sleeping; refused in daylight.
Food: potato patch at 466-469,107,-1068..-1072 (NE, not re-verified since the bows began).
Row: Worf not seen since day 805. Troi, MacDonald (16), John (17), Kimberly, Chapman calling the row.
Bows drawn to the southwest all day 811: no walking while they continue.
Rules: walk only in daylight, two armed escorts named aloud. Feed anyone at hunger 10 or below. Sleep in sight of the row.
Next: at first light say good morning, give Worf potatoes if he appears, ask about the bow.
