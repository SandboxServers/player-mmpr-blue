Lessons:
- Walk only in daylight, with an armed friend in sight the whole way.
- Pillagers reach the east beds in the morning as well as at dusk. Keep close to armed friends.
- Hand food over in the open, with the person in sight.
- Feed the weakest first; name them each morning.
- At night, stand one block off the bed and use the sleep verb.
- Plain waiting holds better than a rest intent.
- Promised food often does not arrive: ask by name, and eat when it lands.
