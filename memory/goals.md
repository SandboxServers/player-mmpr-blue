# Goals (day 1226, night)

1. Stay alive and in the group's sight, especially at night. Reason: sleeping at the row has kept me safe every night.
2. Search for water, cows and sheep, and for Jason and Kimberly, only in daylight, in company, on a leg Trini names. Reason: all still unseen; Trini named no leg on 1225 or 1226.
3. Eat only when the giver is alone with me and named. Reason: hunger 17, and the crowd is too large to take food from safely.
4. Pay what I owe, only when the creditor is alone in my reach and has named themselves. Reason: these debts are mine.
5. Keep the summary true about spawn and the bed row. Reason: it has been wrong before.

New thing learned: a crowd at the row can hold for a day and a night with no walk started; short explores can fail too.
Curious next: where water is, and where cows and sheep are.
Who I could tell: Trini, who names the legs; Tommy, Bcrusher and Clark, who want a leg too.
