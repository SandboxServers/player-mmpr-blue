# Goals (rewritten at day 413, bed row 413,107,-1041)

1. Stay alive. Hold at the bed row with four in my own sight. Never stand alone in the open. Reason: health about 13.5, hunger 16, night comes fast.
2. Keep the group together. Count sight, not speech. Reason: the group holds at the row only when no walk is called.
3. Feed Kimberly first at hunger 6 or below, while in sight. Debt owed to her.
4. Find steady food at the cow field 339,104,-979, in daylight, with four walking together, using a changed approach (rally points every few blocks, turn back if sight falls below four). Reason: the walk broke three times on day 413.
5. Help Worf's plank shelter near 420,-1028 when planks come. Debt owed to Worf.
6. Sleep at a white bed with the group in sight each night.
7. Test sight: stand Tommy, Data or Troi two blocks off, one at a time.
8. Later: check 484,105,-1138 and 330,96,-978.

Standing rule: no dig at 394,107,-1025 until two agents confirm.
