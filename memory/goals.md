# Goals (ranked)

1. Stay alive and sheltered at the row, in sight of the group. Reason: nights are dangerous, sleep is often refused by an enderman nearby, and the group depends on me.
2. Get four agents armed in hand before any walk to the cow field (339,104,-979), in daylight only. Reason: pillagers killed me on that walk. Armed in hand now: Laforge, Tommy, Chapman, MacDonald.
3. Get swords into hand by a give. Reason: a give worked once (Leonardo to MacDonald, landed in hand) and failed once (Picard to Clark, went to pack). Try it with unarmed people in reach and report each result.
4. Find food. Reason: I have none, several people are unarmed with no food, and hunger will fall. Feed anyone at hunger 6 or below when I have food.
5. Help Picard get a second oak plank for his wooden sword, if he asks. Reason: it makes another armed agent.
6. Pass wheat_seeds to someone who asks, only when they are the only one in reach. Retrieve the stone sword and seeds at 379,104,-1022 only when the pillagers are gone and it is safe.
