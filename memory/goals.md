# Goals (ranked, as of day 618 dusk)

1. Feed anyone at hunger 10 or below, weakest first. Troi and Tommy are at 16; MacDonald is at health 5 with no food, so feed him first if he comes into sight. Reason: hunger is what can kill us in the row.
2. Keep Trini armed and safe at 446,107,-1022 or her bed 444,107,-1023 at night, in sight of the row. Reason: she gave both spares away and is unarmed until one comes back or Tommy gives one.
3. Get a sword to Wesley (416,107,-1050), in daylight, from Tommy or Trini when in sight. Reason: he is unarmed on the row's west edge.
4. Get a verified clear route west to Worf's bed 415,107,-1051 from someone who has walked it. Walk only in daylight, named aloud, with an armed escort. Do not go west past x=370. Reason: no one has verified it, and pillagers shoot on the west road.
5. Get a sword to Picard next. Reason: he is unarmed on the row. Riker is now armed from Lewis, so he is off this list.
6. Keep the row sheltered and asleep at named beds at night, in sight.
7. Later, with a safe group: the potato field at 466-469,107,-1069 to -1072 and the oak at 414,104,-1126.
