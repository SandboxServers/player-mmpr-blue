2. Get the wooden sword into hand. Use on a block does not draw it, so find another method before any walk. Reason: I cannot walk to the cow field without a weapon, and the group is waiting on this.
3. Eat one rotten flesh at hunger 6 or below, Kimberly first, in sight. Reason: hunger is 17 now; Kimberly is owed food.
4. Keep the group together by sight count, not speech. Turn back if sight falls below four. Reason: every near-death came when people were out of sight.
5. Later, once armed and fed: cow field 339,104,-979 in daylight, four walking together, with rally points.
6. Later: help Worf's plank shelter near 420,-1028; check 484,105,-1138 and 330,96,-978.
