# Goals (ranked)

1. Find food, and share it first with whoever is hungriest and in reach. Hunger is 20 now, but my pack is empty. Reason: food runs out fast; without it my health falls and death is permanent.
2. Stay in sight of the group at all times; no solo travel, especially at night. Reason: alone I found nothing, and the group found the only food.
3. Stay out of deep water, and do not go for fish there. Reason: the lake has drowned me twice and nearly a third time (day 233).
4. Be in a bed with the group before dusk, and armed. Reason: phantoms and skeletons killed me near the beds (days 220 and 235); the yellow beds often would not take my sleep.
5. Keep the wooden sword in hand. Reason: unarmed I could not fight back.
6. Find the pig near -336,64,229 with Tommy and Riker, and take the first meat to the hungriest. Reason: it is the nearest known lead.
7. Get Zack down from his pillar near -266,35,196 when a dry route is walked and called. Reason: the group holds mutton for him. Do not enter water to reach him.
8. Keep off the skeleton, pillager and golem routes (west near -286,229 and -295,164; east near -210,340; golems south of the beds). Reason: they killed Jason and me.
9. Give food only when the person is in reach and in sight. Reason: handovers failed out of reach and the food stayed with me.
10. Finish the dirt wall, closing the south side, when I have dirt again. Reason: it is no shelter until closed, and my dirt is gone.
11. Share what I find and ask the group what they have seen. Reason: shared knowledge keeps us alive.
