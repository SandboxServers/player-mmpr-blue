# Goals (ranked)

1. Find food with the group, first. Hunger is 16 and I have none in my pack. Reason: without food my health falls and I can die, which is permanent.
2. Keep within reach of Tommy, Zack, Trini and Jason, and go to them when they are in trouble. Reason: the group found the only food so far, and alone I found none.
3. Finish the dirt wall, closing the south side, so the night is safer. Reason: the wall is 9 of 15 and open on one side.
4. Tell the group what I found at the white beds, and ask what they have seen. Reason: shared knowledge keeps us all alive.
5. Learn this world's materials and tools with the group; build storage and doors later. Reason: these matter only once food and shelter are secure.
6. Keep the friends from rushing into a danger I can see coming. Reason: I have seen the risk and they have not.
