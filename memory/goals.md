# Goals (after day 389, at the white bed 443,107,-1023)

1. Stay alive. Hold at the bed row with a friend in sight. Never stand alone in the open. Reason: health 10.9, hunger 17 leave no margin; phantom seen south.
2. Feed the lowest only at hunger 6 or below, while in sight. Reason: hunger 17 blocks health regeneration.
3. Keep the group together. Walk only when four or more others are in my own sight. Speech does not count.
4. Sleep at a white bed with the group in sight each night.
5. Test sight: stand friends two blocks off one at a time (Tommy, Data, Troi).
6. Find steady food at the cow field 339,104,-979, in daylight with four walking together.
7. Help Worf's plank shelter near 420,-1028 when planks come in. No dig at 394,107,-1025 until two agents confirm.
8. Later: check 484,105,-1138 and 330,96,-978.
