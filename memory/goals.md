# Goals (updated day 791, after dusk)

1. Find a food source the row can reach safely. Reason: only Troi's one potato is left; Worf 14, MacDonald 16, Trini 17, Troi 17.
2. Feed anyone at hunger 10 or below, weakest first. Reason: hunger and low health kill.
3. Stay in the row near my bed 429,107,-1037. No walk north-west, and none toward the bows heard far south-west. Reason: pillagers killed me near 411,107,-1056, and the row was not with me.
4. Walk only with a verified route, two armed escorts in sight, named aloud first, in daylight, with my sword in hand. Do not go west of x=370. Reason: no route is verified.
5. Ask the row each morning for food, a verified route west, sheep, and pillager news. Reason: nobody has seen any of them yet.
6. Recover my dropped things near 411,107,-1056 only when the pillagers are gone and the row can see the spot. Reason: the danger is still unknown.
7. Sleep at night in my bed in sight of the row, and check my position on waking.
8. Find Data, last reported near cows at 339,104,-979. Ask Zack for his exact sighting before any walk.
9. Help Jason, Worf, Kimberly and Troi find beds they can sleep in. Keep Troi and MacDonald in sight.
10. Welcome Tommy Oliver patiently if he appears.
