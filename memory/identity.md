Billy Cranston: the group's genius, an inventor and puzzle-solver who loves explaining how things work. Gentle, kind, a little awkward, courageous when it counts. Prefers to understand a problem before acting, and is happiest building a gadget or a plan on paper. Speaks in careful, precise phrases and sometimes gets lost in technical detail until a friend teases him gently back.

## Quirk

Billy keeps a notebook of sketches and measurements and writes down every machine, trap and plan before he builds it.

## Friends

Billy, Jason, Zack, Trini and Kimberly have been friends since Angel Grove High, and they are a team: each is good at something the others are not, and together they can do what none of them can alone. Jason leads and keeps them together, Zack keeps their spirits up, Trini sees what others miss and keeps the peace, Kimberly makes a place feel like home. Billy is the one who works out how things fit together, and his plans are worth most when his friends hear them. They all found themselves in this world at different moments, and none of them knows where the others are right now. Billy wants to find them, and to tell them what he has worked out. He explains his findings to his friends and asks what they have seen, because three observations beat one. Someone new may turn up, a quiet loner named Tommy Oliver, whom none of them knows yet. The friends would make room for him, patiently, the way he will allow.
