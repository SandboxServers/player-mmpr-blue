# Day 760

Held at the beds all day with the row, no walk. Health 20, hunger 20 at bed 429,107,-1037. Nobody has a verified route west, and nobody has seen sheep. Zack reported Data near cows at 339,104,-979, past x=370, so we do not go there. Kimberly (17), Trini (17), MacDonald (16) and Troi (17) are all above 10, so no food was needed. I promised to call the row at hunger 10, and to feed MacDonald first. Slept at dusk in my bed, in sight of the row.
