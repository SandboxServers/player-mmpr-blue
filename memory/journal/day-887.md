# Day 887

Woke at the white bed 443,107,-1065 with health 9.3, hunger 16, six potatoes and no sword. Riker and Troi held at their beds, unarmed. Trini and Leonardo, the armed pair I was waiting for, never came into sight, so no walk to the row was made. Beverly, at health 7, is still unfed and is the one who matters most. At dusk I wrote the handoff and slept at the same bed, not enclosed. Nothing was lost, and no damage was taken.
