# Day 799

Held the row at the beds all day. Health 20, hunger 20 at my bed 429,107,-1037. No food but Lewis's potato, kept for Chapman. Jason scouted the potato route: blocked north-east toward 463,107,-1073, and west shut by oak planks near 415,107,-1050. Picard has not seen the block himself. No walk was named, and none was made. Trini (health 9, hunger 17) and Chapman (health 11, hunger 17) are the weakest; Trini was told to call at hunger 10. No sheep, no pillager news, Data still last near 339,104,-979. Slept at dusk in my bed, in sight of Zack and the row.
