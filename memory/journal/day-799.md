# Day 799

Woke at bed 429,107,-1037, health 20, hunger 20, nothing held. The row held in sight all day; nobody has food beyond Lewis's potato and no one reached hunger 10 yet (Trini 17, John 17, Zack 19, Worf 14). Picard reported ripe potatoes at 463,107,-1073, east of the line, not west of x=370. Worf had not seen them himself. I did not walk. Jason scouted in daylight toward them, found the way blocked, and came back at dusk to his bed in sight. No verified route, sheep or pillager news yet. Went to bed at dusk, in sight of the row.
