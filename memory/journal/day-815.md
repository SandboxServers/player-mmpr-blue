# Day 815

Night came while I was at the beds. Nobody was in danger; health 20, hunger 20. Troi spoke from beside the white bed: Beverly wants a potato, and Troi's own potato is hers if she comes into sight. I held my three potatoes for Worf and did not walk, because the rule is daylight only with escorts. I went to bed at 443,107,-1023 and slept.
