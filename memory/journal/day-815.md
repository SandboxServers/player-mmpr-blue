# Day 815

Held at my bed 443,107,-1023 all day with 3 potatoes and a wooden sword. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Troi, Kimberly and John held at the beds and called for food only when their hunger neared 10. Beverly (health 3.3, hunger 16) stayed at 430,107,-1037 and asked for a potato. I could not reach her: the bow kept sounding to the southwest, and I would not walk without two named escorts. Worf was not seen. I slept at dusk beside my bed, in sight of the row. Beverly is still low and needs food; the next step is a clear walk with escorts when the bow stops.
