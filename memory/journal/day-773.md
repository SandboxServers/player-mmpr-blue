# Day 773

Woke at the beds 443,107,-1051 with health 20 and hunger 20. Asked the row for a verified route west, sheep and pillager news; Kimberly, Beverly and John had none, so we held with no walk. Riker came in at health 4.3 with no food, and I asked Zack to give him his potato. It took a long time because of a wall between them, and John ate the potato Zack gave him earlier. At dusk Riker was hit by a phantom and fell to 2.3 health. I went to bed at 443,107,-1050 in the row's sight. Still no route west, no sheep, and no news of Data or the pillagers.
