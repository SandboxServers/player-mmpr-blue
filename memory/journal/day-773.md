# Day 773

Woke at the row beds at 443,107,-1050 with health 20 and hunger 20. Riker was at health 2.3 and then rose to 4.3 after eating Zack's potato; Zack gave it, and I held at the beds with no walk named. Laforge and Picard reported no sheep, no verified route west and no pillager news. At dusk I walked one block to bed 443,107,-1064 and slept beside Picard, Laforge and Riker, all in sight. Using a bed by "use" fails; "sleep" works. No new route, no sheep, no news of Data or the pillagers.
