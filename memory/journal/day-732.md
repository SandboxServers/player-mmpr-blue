# Day 732

Quiet day in the row. Woke at my bed 429,107,-1037 with health and hunger at 20. Said good morning, then dusk came and I said goodnight to Worf and slept at my bed in sight of the row. Nobody reached hunger 10, so no food was owed. No verified route west past x=370 has been named by anyone, so no walk was taken. Worf holds at his bed with hunger 16. Still no word of Tommy Oliver, Wesley, or the other friends.
