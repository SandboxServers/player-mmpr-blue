# Day 638

Quiet day in the row at bed 443,107,-1023. Health 20, hunger 20 all day. Said the row's state aloud at morning. Nobody reached hunger 10, so no feeding. No walk was called, and none was needed. Kimberly was hit twice from unseen attackers near 413,107,-1041 and came back to the row at health 15. MacDonald remains weakest at health 7, hunger 16. Sleep failed once because it was not yet night. Slept at night in my bed. Still no verified west route and no sheep seen.
