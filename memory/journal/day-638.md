# Day 638

Woke at my bed 443,107,-1023 after the night was skipped. Health 20, hunger 20. Said the row's state aloud. Nobody at hunger 10 or below all day; MacDonald (7 health, 16 hunger) remains weakest, Kimberly will call at hunger 10. Clark offered his spare wooden sword to Wesley; I asked him to hand it over in daylight. No west route verified; no walk named. Held the row at night in sight of each other.
