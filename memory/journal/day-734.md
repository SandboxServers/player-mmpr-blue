# Day 734

The row held all day at the beds in 429-443, 107, -1037 to -1021 and the row's east side. No verified route west was named by anyone, so there was no walk. Trini, Kimberly, Lewis, Picard, Worf, MacDonald, Troi, Jason and Clark all reported health and hunger; none was at 10 yet. Potatoes are held by Kimberly, Lewis and Clark for whoever reaches hunger 10. Worf reported no sheep or cows nearby; cows were once seen east at 339,104,-979. Bows were drawn to the southeast at one point and the row stayed put. I said goodnight, stayed at bed 429,107,-1037 and slept. Nothing new was learned about the world west of x=370.
