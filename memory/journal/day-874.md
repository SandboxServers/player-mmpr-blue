# Day 874 (night)

Nothing much mattered today. I stayed at the bed 430,107,-1050 at night with health 20 and hunger 20. Kimberly and Bcrusher stood near, both holding potatoes, but neither was in reach for a give. I said aloud that I had 2 potatoes for whoever was weakest, and nobody answered. A bed use failed because the block is a bed, so I used the sleep call instead. I slept and nothing new happened. Tomorrow: ask who is weakest, give only to those in reach, and walk west only with an armed friend in sight.
