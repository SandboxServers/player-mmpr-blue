# Day 633

The row held at its beds all day. Everyone reported in healthy: health 20 for most, Tommy 15, MacDonald 7, Troi 14, hunger mostly 16 to 20. I called a daylight west look to 413,107,-1050 with Picard and Trini as escort. It failed after a few steps when a bow was drawn to the southwest, so I turned back to my bed. No one has a verified west route yet, and no sheep has been seen. I slept at my bed in the row.
