# Day 633

Stayed in the row all day at my bed 443,107,-1023. Said my state aloud, no west walk was named because no route is verified and a bow was seen to the southwest. Lewis, Riker, Tommy, Troi and MacDonald held at their beds; MacDonald (health 7) and Tommy and Troi (health 14-15) are the ones to feed at hunger 10. Picard and Trini stayed at their beds. Went to sleep at night at my bed in sight of the row; woke with 7 of 10 needed asleep. Nothing new learned; no sheep seen; still no west route.
