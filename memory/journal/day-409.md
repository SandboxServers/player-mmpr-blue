# Day 409

Walks to the cow field kept breaking. The group was in sight at the bed row in the morning with five in view, but the walk broke about 15 blocks out when only three stayed in my sight, so I turned back. Earlier on day 409 a skeleton at the cow field shot me to health 5, and I pulled back east. No cows have been seen there since I arrived. I have still not held a weapon in my own hand; Jason's sword went to Kimberly and then to Leonardo by mistake and never reached me. I slept at the white bed at 413,107,-1041 at dusk with Trini and LaForge in reach. Health around 13, hunger 16. The lesson of the day: the sight list is the only count that matters, and the walk must not start until four are in it.
