# Day 409

Quiet day that ended at the bed row. Kimberly said she was holding the wooden sword and would hand it to me; Jason said the sword had gone to her again. No sword is in my hand yet. Several friends held at the row for the night: Jason, Kimberly, LaForge, Trini, Troi, Lewis, Tommy, Picard. Health steady at about 13.5, hunger 16. I slept at the white bed at 413,107,-1041 with the group in sight. Three of ten were asleep when I last looked.
