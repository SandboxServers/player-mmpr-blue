# Day 409

Held at the bed row near 409,107,-1041 through the afternoon after two failed cow walks. At dusk, six were in sight (Trini, LaForge, Tommy, Troi, Bcrusher, Kimberly). I walked to the white bed at 413,107,-1041 and slept there with Trini and LaForge in reach. The first sleep did not take; the second one did. Nothing hurt me. Health 13.5 and hunger 16 at sleep. No sword in hand, so no walk. Next: count the group at first light, feed Kimberly if her hunger is 6 or below, and wait for four in sight before any walk.
