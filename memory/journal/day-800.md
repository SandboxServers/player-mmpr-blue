# Day 800

Held at bed 429,107,-1037 all day, in sight of the row. Health 20, hunger 20, nothing held. No walk. Nobody has seen the exact blocked tile on the north-east route; Jason's notes give only 463,107,-1073. No verified route, so no walk. MacDonald (hunger 16) and Worf (14) have no food; I have nothing to give. Bows reported to the south. Slept at dusk in sight of the row.
