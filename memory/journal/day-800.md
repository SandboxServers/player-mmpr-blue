# Day 800

Slept at bed 429,107,-1037 in sight of the row, health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing. Picard, MacDonald and Worf all said good night and held their places, with no walk. MacDonald is at health 15, hunger 16, with no food. Worf is at hunger 14. Nothing new was found; the potato route and the blocked tile are still unknown. I said goodnight aloud and slept.
