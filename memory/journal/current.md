# Journal
- Day 616-617: held in the row at bed 443,107,-1051, health 20, hunger 20. Named Riker's daylight walk east to Trini for a sword. MacDonald at health 5, hunger 17, no food; Troi has 8 potatoes and will share if he comes into sight. Pillagers still shooting in the west and south. Still no verified west route; no walk named. Dusk now: going to sleep in my bed in sight of the row.
