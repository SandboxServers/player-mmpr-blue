# Handoff (day 1221, dusk)

At -952,73,-3047 at the bed row, in the group's sight, not enclosed, not walking. Health 20, hunger 17. No errand running, no danger.

No leg named for me by Trini or Mulan on 1220 or 1221. Mulan's shelter box at -955,72,-3051 is partly up (about 18 blocks), plan paused; I am not part of it. No water, cows or sheep found yet.

Owed (unchanged): 2 sticks to Tommy (when alone with me, named). 1 stick to Doc via Brunel. 5 oak planks to Ripley (alone, named). 2 rotten flesh to the weakest who names themselves alone.

Night: no walks in the dark. Sleep at a bed in the row, in group sight. First light: hold at the row until Trini or Mulan names a daylight leg with company.
