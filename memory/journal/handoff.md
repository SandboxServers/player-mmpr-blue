# Handoff (day 1217, night)

At -950,73,-3047 at the bed row, in the group's sight, not enclosed, not walking. Health 20, hunger 17. No errand running, no danger. No water or cows found yet. No daylight leg was named by Trini today; I held at the row all morning.

Owed (unchanged): 5 oak planks to Ripley, only when she stands alone in my reach and names herself. 2 sticks to Tommy. 1 stick to Doc via Brunel. 2 rotten flesh to the weakest who names themselves alone.

Chewie offered a carrot. I declined in the crowd; I take food only when the giver is alone with me.

Night: no walks in the dark. Sleep at the bed in the row, in group sight. First light: hold at the row until Trini names a leg with company.
