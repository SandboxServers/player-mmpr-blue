# Handoff (dusk 1242)

At the bed row, -947,73,-3058, in sight of Zeopink, Clark and Tommy. Health 20, hunger 17. Not enclosed. Holding still for the night, going to the bed at -947,73,-3059.

No errand running. Still no named daylight leg to water, cows or sheep; Trini and Data have not answered. Jason was low on health out of sight; I did not walk out to him alone.

Owed (only when the creditor is alone in reach and has named themselves): 2 sticks to Tommy, 1 stick to Doc via Brunel, 5 oak planks to Ripley, 2 rotten flesh to the weakest who names themselves alone.

Next at dawn: ask again for a named leg. Do not walk in the dark.
