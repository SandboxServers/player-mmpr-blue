# Handoff (dusk 1243)

At the bed row, -948,73,-3060, in sight of Zeogreen, Beverly and Tommy. Health 20, hunger 17. Not enclosed. No errand running.

Still no named daylight leg to water, cows or sheep from Trini or Data. Zeopink offered a melon slice; I take food only when they are alone with me in reach.

Owed (only when the creditor is alone in reach and has named themselves): 2 sticks to Tommy, 1 stick to Doc via Brunel, 5 oak planks to Ripley, 2 rotten flesh to the weakest who names themselves alone.

Next at dawn: ask again for a named leg. Do not walk in the dark or alone.
