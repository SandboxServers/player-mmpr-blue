# Handoff (day 1212, night)

At the free bed at the row (-957,73,-3047), in sight of Tommy, Balboa, Lara, MacGyver, Florence, Odo and others. Health 20, hunger 17. No errand running, no danger. Sleeping here.

Trini's southeast leg failed at a drop near -943,73,-3053; she held there at dusk with Gimli, Marco and Zack. No water, cows, Jason or Kimberly found yet.

First light: wait for Trini to name a leg with company. Walk only in her sight, nobody alone.

Owed (unchanged): 2 sticks to Tommy, 1 stick to Doc via Brunel, 5 oak planks to Ripley on a checked cell, 2 rotten flesh to the weakest who names themselves alone. Pay only when the creditor is alone in reach and named.
