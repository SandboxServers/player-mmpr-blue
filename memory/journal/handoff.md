# Handoff, day 813 dusk

Billy at bed 443,107,-1023 beside the row. Health 20, hunger 20. Holding 3 potatoes (for Worf) and a wooden sword. No errand half-done.
Worf not seen yet. Give him the potatoes the moment he is in sight.
Beverly at 429,107,-1035, health 3, hunger 16, with MacDonald; she will eat his potato. Give her one of mine only if she is in sight and hunger is 10 or below.
John at bed 429,107,-1022. Kimberly and Troi near 444,107,-1037.
Bow still drawn to the southwest. No walking while it continues.
Next morning: good morning, ask the row about Worf, the bow and food.
