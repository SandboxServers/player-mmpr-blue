# Handoff (night 1245)

At the bed row, -948,73,-3060, in sight of Tommy and the group. Health 20, hunger 17. Not enclosed. No errand running.

Still no named daylight leg to water, cows or sheep. Zeopink holds a melon for me; I take it only when they are alone with me in reach.

Owed (only when creditor is alone in reach and has named themselves): 2 sticks to Tommy, 1 stick to Doc via Brunel, 5 oak planks to Ripley, 2 rotten flesh to the weakest who names themselves alone.

Next at dawn: ask Trini or Data for a named leg. Do not walk in the dark or alone.
