# Day 390

Night at the white bed 443,107,-1023 with Jason in reach and the row in sight. Health steady near 10.9, hunger 17, so no food and no walk. Morning counted the row: Jason, Tommy, Trini, Data, Troi, Lewis, Riker, Picard, Kimberly. Kimberly is the lowest at health 6.3 and Riker at 8.4. Holding at the bed row; no walk until four or more are in my sight and I call it. Slept at the white bed and woke with nothing new to act on.
