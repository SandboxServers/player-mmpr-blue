# Day 643

Morning at my bed 443,107,-1023 in the row. Health 20, hunger 20. The row held all day with no walk named: Trini, Picard, Tommy, MacDonald, Deanna, Lewis, Clark. Nobody fell to hunger 10 or below. MacDonald stayed weakest at health 7, hunger 17, holding five potatoes; Deanna at 13.9 health, hunger 16. Clark holds a sword for Wesley and potatoes for the hungry. No west route verified, no sheep seen, Wesley not seen, and Jason has not yet answered. I said the row's state aloud, asked the group what they have seen, and kept everyone at their beds in sight. Slept at dusk at my bed.
