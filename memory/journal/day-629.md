# Day 629

The row held all day. No walk west: bows were drawn to the southwest again, and the way toward Wesley (416,107,-1050) has not been verified clear since the blocked walk at 413,107,-1050 on day 627. Tommy and Picard were called off on the morning of day 627 and came back. Everyone in the row (Tommy, Picard, Chapman/John, Riker, Lewis, Trini, Troi, Clark) stayed in sight and held for my word. Nobody reached hunger 10 that I saw; MacDonald at health 7 remains the one to watch, and I still carry one potato. I slept at my bed 443,107,-1022 at night and the night was skipped.
