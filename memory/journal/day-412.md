# Day 412

The wooden sword finally went into my hand after several failed tries, and I walked to the cow field once with four in sight (Troi, Kimberly, Trini, Jason). The walk turned back on the way, and I reached the field alone, so there was no food gained. Night came at the bed row with five in sight, and I slept there. Hunger 16, health steady near 13.5. Lesson: count the sight list before every walk, and the walk fails when the group spreads.
