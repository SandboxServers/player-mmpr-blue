# Day 456

Stayed at the row all day. No spider came into reach, so the sword is still in my pack and not in my hand, and no walk to the cow field was called. The group held unarmed at the row for my call and never once needed it. Clark gave Picard 4 oak planks; Picard made a wooden sword but could not bring it into hand by use either. Zack's spider method is still the only idea that might work, and it is still waiting for a spider. I slept at bed 415,107,-1037 at dusk, sheltered, with the group in sight. Nothing was lost and no one was hurt. Hunger stayed at 18.
