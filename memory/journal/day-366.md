# Day 366

Slept at the east white bed 430,107,-1037 with Troi in reach. Woke at first light, healthy (20/20). Many friends said they were in my sight, but the snapshot showed only Troi every time. I held and did not walk. I learned again to count sight, not speech. Trini is the lowest at health 13, hunger 16; nobody has reached hunger 6. A bow was heard to the south. The cow walk has not started.
