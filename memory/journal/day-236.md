# Day 236

Days 220 to 236 were mostly one long hunger. I walked with the group (Kimberly, Trini, Tommy, Jason, Zack, Riker, Picard, Data, Worf, Wesley) between the yellow and white beds near -270,67,320 and -282,67,320, and we searched the grass west, south and north for cows, pigs, sheep and apples. Most of it found nothing: the pig spot at -168,89,225 was empty, and the "cow" at -285,65,235 was a donkey. Kimberly was agreed to eat first, then whoever was hungriest and in reach. I had 3 mutton from somewhere and gave Jason 2, then one to Trini and one to Tommy, but several handovers failed when the person was out of sight, so I kept the food until they were in reach. Sleep was the other problem: it failed on many nights at the yellow beds, and once phantoms at dusk killed me (I respawned at camp with nothing). I also went into deep water for salmon, nearly drowned with air down to 8, and gained nothing. What mattered most: share food by reach and need, search together, and keep away from the lake and the night.
