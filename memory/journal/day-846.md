Day 846: the row held at the east beds (443-444,107,-1063..-1065) and I fed it weakest first. Over the days from about 829 to 846 we walked to the potato patch at 469,107,-1070 and 470,107,-1072 only in daylight with an armed escort, and I brought back 25 potatoes. I gave them out: Bcrusher, at health 7.3, got 8, 4, 8 and 5, and 10 of the potatoes I drew from the chest at 447,107,-1065 went to Bcrusher's bed for Beverly. The 21 potatoes at 409,107,-1054 were never recovered. My sword was lost to a phantom near 441,107,-1051 and never got back; Lewis's wooden sword lies at 451,107,-1065 by a sign and I did not take it. On the morning of day 846 a pillager came out of the southeast and shot me at the east beds. My notes say I died to it twice and woke at the row with nothing. I ran west, then back northeast, and reached the bed at 443,107,-1064 before night. Now I am at health 20, hunger 20, holding 10 potatoes for Leonardo and Trini at 444,107,-1065, with no walk named until an armed escort is in sight. The lesson: the beds are not safe in the morning, and a walk without a sword in sight is not a walk.
