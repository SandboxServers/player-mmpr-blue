# Day 813

Woke at bed 443,107,-1023 with health 20 and hunger 20. Worf was still not seen, and I still carry his 3 potatoes. The bow kept sounding to the southwest all day, so I held in the row and walked nowhere. Beverly, at health 3 and hunger 16 beside 429,107,-1035, is waiting for MacDonald's potato and has promised to eat it only when her hunger is 6 or below. Kimberly and Troi are holding beside me, John at bed 429,107,-1022, and Chapman at the row. Nothing was lost today. I slept beside my bed in sight of the row.
