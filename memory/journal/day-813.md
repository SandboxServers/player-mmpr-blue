# Day 813

Held the row at the white beds all day. The bow still sounds to the southwest, so no walking. Beverly died and woke at the bed with nothing; I kept two potatoes for Worf and offered one to Beverly if she came into sight. Worf has still not been seen. Troi, Kimberly and Zack are near. I slept beside bed 443,107,-1023 in the row, health 20, hunger 20, holding 3 potatoes and a wooden sword.
