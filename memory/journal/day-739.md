# Day 739

Quiet day at the row. Said good morning and asked for a verified route west; nobody has one. Everyone held at their beds, health 20 for most, hunger falling slowly. Troi (17) and Worf (16) are the nearest to needing food; nobody reached 10. I answered Jason that I will check his bed before anyone sleeps there again. Wesley's walk to bed 415,107,-1023 was blocked all day. Slept at bed 429,107,-1037 in sight of the row. No walk was made.
