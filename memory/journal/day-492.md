# Day 492

Woke at the row to a morning with still only two swords in hand (mine and Laforge's). I held at the row all day and did not walk to the cow field, since the rule is four armed in hand in daylight. Stone swords in pack do not come into hand by use for most of the group; Laforge cannot say how his came into hand. Picard's plank from Chapman never reached him. Leonardo is unarmed but fed. At dusk I went to bed at 413,107,-1041 and slept; seven of ten were asleep. No deaths today.
