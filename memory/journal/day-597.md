# Day 597

Held in the row at bed 444,107,-1037 all day, health 20, hunger 20. Nobody walked. Troi (hunger 16) and Tommy (hunger 16) are waiting to eat at 10; I hold one potato for them. Trini is at 415,107,-1035, not her named bed, and will not walk without my named call. Kimberly, Worf and Wesley's bed refusals are still unresolved. No west route has been verified. At dusk I slept in bed 443,107,-1037 and it worked, so the row is sleeping in sight.
