# Day 763

Quiet day in the row at bed 429,107,-1037. Health 20, hunger 20 at sleep. Nobody at hunger 10 or below; MacDonald lowest at 16, Worf 14, Troi 17, Kimberly 17. Everyone holding, no walk named. Still no verified route west past x=370 and no sheep seen by anyone in the row. Lewis, Beverly, John and Wesley all reported the same. Clark (William) offered 5 potatoes to whoever is hungriest. Heard a bow drawn to the southwest, nobody hurt. I said good morning, asked again for a route and sheep, and went to bed at dusk.

Next: at first light, say good morning, check hunger, feed anyone at 10 or below, ask again about the western route and sheep. Do not walk without a named call and two armed escorts in daylight.
