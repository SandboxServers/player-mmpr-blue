# Day 720

Quiet day at the row. I said good morning twice and asked again for a verified route past x=370. Picard, Kimberly, Lewis, Beverly and Troi all said they have seen none, so no walk was made. Everyone's hunger stayed above 10; Kimberly held at my side at health 15, hunger 17, and said she will call at 10. Bows were drawn to the southwest many times, far off. At dusk Lewis went to his bed at 443,107,-1036 and I slept at mine, 443,107,-1023; the sleep began with 5 of 10 needed asleep.
