Day 660. Woke at my bed 443,107,-1023 in the row, health 20, hunger 20. No walk was named all day. Kimberly holds the potato; Tommy, Troi and Deanna hovered near hunger 16, MacDonald at health 11. Nobody reached hunger 10, so no feeding was needed. Clark, Picard, Kimberly and Beverly all gathered or said they would go to the middle chest 447,107,-1037 when I name an escort, but I did not name one, because no armed escort was ready before dusk. Lewis and Beverly are unarmed. Dusk came on day 661 and the row slept in sight at their beds.
