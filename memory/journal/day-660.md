Day 660. Morning in the row, everyone at beds in sight, health 11-20, hunger 16-20, nobody at hunger 10. Kimberly holds the potato. I named a short daylight west check to x=380 (not past 370) with Billy, Picard and Clark armed. Clark said he was ready at his bed, Picard was at 416,107,-1037 and could not see us, and MacDonald, Tommy and Bcrusher stayed unarmed at their beds. The walk never started because the escort never came into one sight. At dusk I slept at my bed 443,107,-1023. The west route is still unverified and no sheep, Wesley or bows seen this day.
