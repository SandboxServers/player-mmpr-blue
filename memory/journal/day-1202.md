# Day 1202

Woke at the bed row, health 20, hunger 18, no food in my pack. Nemo and Bilbo were lower, so I did not ask for food. Stayed in group sight with Zack, Bilbo, Zeogreen and others. Told Tommy I would pay his 2 sticks only when he and I stand alone within reach and he names himself; he was not alone, so I did not pay. Jason and Kimberly still unseen. Nothing given, nothing eaten, no one hurt. The day was spent holding at the row and keeping the rules: no walks alone, no walks in the dark.
