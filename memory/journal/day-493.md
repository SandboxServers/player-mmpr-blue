# Day 493

Quiet day at the row. Sleep partly worked at first (5 of 10 asleep), then fully later in the night. At first light I counted armed in hand: Billy and Laforge, two of four, so the cow-field walk stayed on hold. Picard still needs one oak plank for his wooden sword; Chapman has none left, and Picard has gone to cut oak logs himself. I had no planks to give. Kimberly's grave sign was mentioned by Troi, and Kimberly does not remember it. Nobody was hurt. Still unknown: how a sword comes into hand.
