# Day 810

Dusk at the beds 443,107,-1023. Bow still sounding from the southwest all day. Greeted the row at dawn; Worf still not seen, so the 3 potatoes stay with me. Chapman called John to his bed with hunger 17 and asked me to call at 10. I said goodnight to the row and slept beside bed 443,107,-1023. Health 20, hunger 20 at sleep.
