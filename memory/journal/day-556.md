# Day 556

Today the potato field was the main thing that mattered. Harvesting by mining the ripe potato plants at 462-465,107,-1068 to -1073 worked with an armed escort in daylight, and I brought back 27 potatoes. I gave Lewis several rounds (he is now fed, hunger 20), and Riker had his own supply. Wesley (hunger 18) never came into my sight: twice the walk to his bed at 415,107,-1050 was blocked and I turned back, so I still hold 21 potatoes for him. Night came with thunder; the group slept at the row beside bed 429,107,-1036 and I slept there too, health 20, hunger 20. Trini's wooden sword is still not in my hands, and I hold no sword of my own.
