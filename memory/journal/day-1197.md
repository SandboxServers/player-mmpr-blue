# Day 1197

Food stayed short all day. The group rations melon and bread to the lowest hunger, and I held my 2 rotten flesh. Jason and Kimberly were still not seen. I walked west with Zack and Fogg looking for cows and found none. Health stayed at 20 and no danger came. At dusk I went to a bed at the row, in the group's sight, and tried to sleep; the sleep was short. Tommy still waits for my 2 sticks, but Zack and Fogg were always in reach, so I did not give them. I wrote the handoff note with the owed list: 2 sticks to Tommy, 1 stick to Doc via Brunel, 5 oak planks to Ripley, 2 rotten flesh to the weakest who names himself alone.
