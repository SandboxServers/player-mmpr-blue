# Day 640

Held at my bed 443,107,-1023 in the row all day, health 20, hunger 20. Nobody reached hunger 10 or below, so no feeding was needed. Troi reported bows to the south; we did not walk, and no walk was named. MacDonald (health 7, hunger 16) stayed at his bed in sight. Kimberly, hit from behind near 413,-1041 the night before, stayed close to Trini and me. At dusk I called the row's state aloud, then slept. Sleep was partial at first (3 of 10 asleep), then worked at night. Still no verified west route and no sheep seen.
