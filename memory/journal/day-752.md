# Day 752

Nothing changed today. I held at my bed at 429,107,-1051 with the row all day. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout; nobody reached hunger 10. Every holder (Worf, Jason, Zack, Kimberly, Trini, Wesley, Picard, Chapman, Beverly, Lewis, Laforge) reported no verified route west, so no walk was named and none was taken. Trini asked after Tommy Oliver; I have not seen him. Wesley asked for a food route, and I had none to give. At dusk I slept beside my bed in sight of the row; the sleep was 9 of 10 asleep when the wait returned, so the night was mostly kept.
