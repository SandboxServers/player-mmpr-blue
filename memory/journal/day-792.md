# Day 792

Short look east done with Jason and Worf to 439,107,-1037. Only Chapman seen; no food, no sheep, no news. The row held at the beds in sight all day, hunger 14 to 20. Trini, Troi, Worf and MacDonald are the ones closest to needing food; Troi holds the only potato. No pillager news and no verified route west. Went to bed at dusk in my bed at 429,107,-1037, in the row's sight, health 20, hunger 20.
