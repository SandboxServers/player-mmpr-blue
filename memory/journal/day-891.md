# Day 891

Night at the white bed 443,107,-1022 with Trini and Kimberly near and John (Chapman) at the bed beside me. Health 13.3, hunger 17, two potatoes held for Worf and a wooden sword in hand. Bows were heard to the southwest all evening, so nobody walked. Beverly, Worf and Zack were still out of sight, so the potato debts stand. The sleep call did not clearly confirm a night's rest, so the bed may need trying again at dawn. Lesson: keep to the beds at night, and feed the weakest first when someone is in sight.
