# Day 774

Held at the beds near 444,107,-1065 with the row all day. No walk was named: no verified route west, no sheep, no pillager news. Riker fell to health 2.3 after a phantom attack and was fed one potato by Zack, who was slow to reach him because a plank wall kept them out of sight of each other; he is now at 4.3 and rising. Chapman is at health 11, hunger 17, and Laforge, Picard and Beverly are at full health with no food. I had nothing to give. At dusk I slept beside the beds, and the row slept with me in sight.
