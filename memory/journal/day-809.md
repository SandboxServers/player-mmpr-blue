# Day 809

Held still all day at the white beds, no walking, because the bow kept being drawn to the southwest. Worf has still not been seen. The row is MacDonald (15/16, 3 potatoes), Troi (14/17, 2 potatoes), John (11/17, no food, no sword), and Kimberly (20/20, no food). Nobody has reached hunger 10 yet except John is close, so I kept my 3 potatoes for Worf. Slept beside bed 443,107,-1023 in sight of the row.
