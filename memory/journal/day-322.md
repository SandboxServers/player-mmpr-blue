# Day 322

My chicken went to Jason by mistake on the give, then reached Kimberly through him; she is fed. Trini gave me a chicken, which I am holding for Leonardo (hunger 10) or Johnny. Tommy searches the north-east chicken field. Jason, Kimberly, Trini, Leonardo and Tommy are the group at the white beds. I slept at the bed at 443,107,-1036 at night. Johnny and Zack are still not seen; bows are drawn to the south and south-west.
