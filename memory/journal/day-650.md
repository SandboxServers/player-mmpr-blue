# Day 650 (night)

Held at my bed 443,107,-1023 in the row all day. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. Nobody at hunger 10 or below; no walk named, no west route verified, so no walk. Kimberly and Tommy stayed in sight at the row. Said the row state aloud, noted it, then slept. Nothing new learned about the west or the bows. Wesley still not seen.
