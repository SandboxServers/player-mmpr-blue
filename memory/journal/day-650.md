# Day 650

Night at my bed 443,107,-1023 in the row. Woke at night with health and hunger full (20/20). Morning came with the whole row holding at beds, waiting for me to name a walk. No walk was named: no verified west route, and nobody goes west of x=370. MacDonald (health 9, hunger 17) ate a potato as I asked. Nobody reached hunger 10 or below. Clark holds the spare sword for Wesley, who has not been seen. Kimberly will not walk alone. Slept at dusk in my bed, with the row asleep in sight. Nothing new learned beyond the row's state.
