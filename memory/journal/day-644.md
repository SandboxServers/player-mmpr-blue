# Day 644

Woke at my bed in the row, health 20, hunger 20. The row held at their beds in my sight all day and waited for my word on a walk. I named no walk, since there is still no verified route west and we do not go west of x=370. I said the row's state aloud, asked Jason about the southern bows (no answer yet), and told MacDonald (health 7) to eat. Nobody was at hunger 10 or below. Slept at my bed at night; Tommy and Lewis are sleeping in sight.
