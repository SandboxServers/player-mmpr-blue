# Day 876

The day went on with talk and waiting. I gave my 2 potatoes to Leonardo, who was weakest in reach, and then asked Kimberly to give Beverly 4 and Trini 2 from her 6. Beverly is still the weakest at health 7 and has not yet been fed. Bcrusher holds 10 potatoes and owes Zack 3 and Worf 5, but gives only when they are in his sight. Nobody can walk west safely yet, since no armed friend is with us. I slept in bed at 430,107,-1051 at night. Everyone in the row is holding, and they are waiting for me to name a daylight walk.
