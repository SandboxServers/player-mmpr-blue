# Day 617

Held in the row all day at bed 443,107,-1051, health 20, hunger 20. Pillagers shot people in the west and south: Wesley and Kimberly died to arrows on the west road and woke at the row spawn; Riker died to pillagers at 413,-1061 and his sword lies there. MacDonald fell to health 5 and has no food; Troi holds 8 potatoes and will share if he comes into sight. Tommy and Troi are at hunger 16, not yet at the call of 10. I named one daylight walk, Riker east to Trini at 444,107,-1023 for a sword, with Trini armed in place. No verified west route exists yet. Slept at dusk in my bed, in sight of the row.
