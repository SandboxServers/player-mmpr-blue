# Day 743

Morning greeting drew replies from the row. Nobody was at hunger 10 or below; the lowest were Trini, Troi and Kimberly at 17. Zack and Lewis hold potatoes for anyone who comes into their sight, and MacDonald (health 15, hunger 16) was sent to them; he reached Zack's sight and ate. Jason, Picard, William, Beverly and Worf all hold at beds in sight. Nobody has seen a verified route west, so there was no walk. I held at bed 429,107,-1037 all day and slept there at dusk in sight of the row.
