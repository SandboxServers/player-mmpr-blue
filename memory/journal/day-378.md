# Day 378

Day mattered for the walk, not the cows. I held at the bed row all day, counting sight only. Morning: four in sight (Riker, Kimberly, Wesley, Jason) and we set out for the cow field at 339,104,-979, but the group fell out of sight and I stopped with only Kimberly near. We walked back with her in view at dusk. I slept at the white bed 401,107,-1022 with no one else in sight. Still holding only a wooden sword; no food. Lesson: the walk is only safe when the four stay in my sight the whole way, and I should not start it late in the day. Next: first light, count four, cow walk with the group, me last.
