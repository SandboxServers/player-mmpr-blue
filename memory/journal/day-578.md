# Day 578

Mostly holding at the row. Gave Zack potatoes twice (hunger 17, now eating). Named beds for Worf (415,107,-1023) and Kimberly (429,107,-1036); Jason keeps 415,107,-1051, Tommy 443,107,-1051, Riker 443,107,-1022, Lewis my bed neighbour at 443,107,-1037. Deanna at health 8 holds 9 potatoes and was told to eat. Dusk came before I could name every bed; I went to my bed at 443,107,-1037 with Lewis in reach and slept there.
