# Day 719

Quiet day at my bed in the row. Health and hunger stayed at 20 for me; Kimberly, Troi and Trini stayed in the teens, none reached hunger 10. I greeted the row each morning and asked again for a verified route past x=370. Nobody has seen one, and nobody has walked since they came. Bows were drawn to the southwest many times and nothing came of it. I named no walk, so no one left. I slept at my bed 443,107,-1023 with Kimberly and Lewis in sight.
