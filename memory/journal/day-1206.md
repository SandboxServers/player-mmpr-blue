# Day 1206

Stayed at the bed row all day, in the group's sight. Health 20 throughout; hunger drifted from 18 to 18 with no food taken. Nobody stood alone with me, so I gave and received nothing; the rule held. Zack and I agreed to share any trace of Jason or Kimberly; none found yet. Bilbo got melon from Sattler, MacGyver got melon from Aragorn and chicken from Bilbo. My bed at -958,73,-3047 was taken before I came back to it at dusk, and I slept in it at night. Owed items unchanged: 2 sticks to Tommy, 1 stick to Doc via Brunel, 5 oak planks to Ripley, 2 rotten flesh to the weakest who names themselves alone.
