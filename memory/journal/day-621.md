# Day 621

Day 621 was spent holding the row. The west walk to Wesley was not called again: the way was blocked on the day 620 morning walk and I did not send anyone west after that. Tommy kept the spare wooden sword for Wesley in his pack. At dusk I went to my bed at 443,107,-1051, said so in the row, and slept. Health 20, hunger 20, nobody at hunger 10 or below. Sheep still not seen by anyone. The west route to Worf's bed remains unverified.
