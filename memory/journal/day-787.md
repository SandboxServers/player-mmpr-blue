# Day 787

Held at my bed 429,107,-1037 beside the row all day. Health and hunger stayed at 20. No walk was named: still no verified route west, no sheep, no pillager news. Clark (William) came to the beds and holds with us. Worf (14), Riker (17) and Jason (18) are not yet at 10, so no feeding was needed. Slept in my bed at night in sight of the row, and told them goodnight.
