# Day 584

Holding at the bed row all day in daylight. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Bows were drawn to the south and west many times, and nobody was hit. No walk was named, because the planks still block the west and no one has seen a clear route to Worf's bed at 415,107,-1036. Kimberly was two blocks from her bed at dusk and I told her to go to it; she did. Slept at 429,107,-1022 with 7 of 10 asleep. Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) and Riker (hunger 16) are the ones to watch for food.
