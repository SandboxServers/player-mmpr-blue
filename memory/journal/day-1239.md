# Day 1239

Stayed at the bed row, -944,73,-3060, all day. Nobody named a water or animal leg until Trini's west leg to -990,73,-3047 was offered at dawn 1237; I did not walk it. Said I would walk in daylight in sight of Trini, Zack, Jason and Zeogreen, and I did not reach that leg. Several people (Zeopink, Clark, Tommy, Beverly) have no food; I hold 2 rotten flesh, owed to the weakest who names themselves alone. Hunger 17 all day, health steady at 20. Slept at the row in company at dusk 1239, not enclosed.
