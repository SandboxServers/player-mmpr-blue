Day 705. Woke at my bed in the row, health 20, hunger 20. Said good morning and told the row to hold: no walk named, no verified route. Deanna at hunger 16, holding her potato for Kimberly's call at 10. Bows heard southwest all day, nothing came near. Trini, Lewis, Beverly, Deanna all reported holding at their beds. Slept at dusk in my bed, in sight of the row. No new route, no sheep, no Wesley or Tommy seen.
