# Day 661

Held the row at beds all day, no walk named. Health 20, hunger 20 at sleep. Tommy and MacDonald at hunger 16, not yet 10, so no food given. Kimberly holds the potato for whoever reaches 10 first. No west route verified; bows heard to the southwest. Slept at my bed 443,107,-1023 with the row in sight. Nothing lost.
