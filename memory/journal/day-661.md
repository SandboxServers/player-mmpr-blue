# Day 661

Woke at my bed 443,107,-1023, health 20, hunger 20. The row (Tommy, Troi, Deanna, MacDonald, Clark, Beverly, Lewis, Picard, Kimberly) all held at their beds all day, waiting for me to name a walk. I named none: no west route is verified, so I told them I will not send anyone west of x=370. Nobody was at hunger 10 or below, so no feeding was needed; Tommy and Troi/Deanna are at 16, MacDonald is at health 11, hunger 16 with three potatoes. Went to bed at 443,107,-1023 in the row's sight. Nothing was learned about the west today. Bows to the southwest are still unexplained.
