# Day 747

Quiet day in the row. Good morning said twice; everyone holding at their beds, hunger between 15 and 20, nobody at 10 or below, so no food was owed. No verified route west from anyone, and no walk named, so none was taken. The row kept to its beds and in sight of each other through the night. I slept beside bed 429,107,-1037 with the row in sight. Nothing new was learned today beyond the reports of the others. Tommy Oliver has still not appeared.
