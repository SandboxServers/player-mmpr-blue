# Day 747

Beside bed 429,107,-1037 with the row in sight all day and night. Health 20, hunger 20 at sleep. Nobody in the row is at hunger 10 or below; lowest is Troi, Trini and Kimberly near 17. No verified route west from anyone, so no walk was named and none was taken. Went to sleep in sight of the row. Still waiting on a verified route west and on Tommy Oliver. Holding everyone in sight is the work for now.
