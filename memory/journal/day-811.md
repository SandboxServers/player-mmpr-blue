# Day 811

Held still at bed 443,107,-1023 all day with the row: Kimberly, John (Chapman), MacDonald, Troi, Zack. The bow kept sounding from the southwest, so nobody walked. Worf did not appear, so the 3 potatoes are still in my pack for him. Everyone's health and hunger were holding (MacDonald 15/16, Troi 14/17, the rest 20). Nothing was learned new today beyond that the row is steady. Slept at night beside the bed in sight of the row. Tomorrow: good morning, ask about Worf and the bow, and walk only if the bow stops and two armed escorts are named.
