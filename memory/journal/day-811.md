# Day 811

Woke at 444,107,-1023 beside the white bed, health 20, hunger 20. Said good morning to the row and asked about Worf and the bow. MacDonald (health 15, hunger 16) and John (hunger 17) hold at their beds; Kimberly and Troi hold at 444,107,-1037. Nobody has seen Worf since day 805, so the 3 potatoes stay with me for him. The bow was drawn to the southwest several times through the day, so I did not walk. Nothing hurt me. At dusk I slept beside the bed in sight of the row.
