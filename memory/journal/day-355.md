# Day 355

Woke at the bed row near 402,-1034 with Kimberly, Picard and Tommy in reach, health 20, hunger 20. The cow walk to hop one 395,-1001 stalled a third time: I walked a short hop to 396,-1010 alone, found nobody in sight, and came back. Nobody was hungry enough to need food, so the group held at the bed row all day. At dusk I went into the white bed at 401,-1036 with the group near and slept; the sleep worked (5 of 10 asleep) and I woke at day. Lesson: the group says ready but does not follow; call the walk only when several are visibly in reach, and keep the rule of never walking alone.
