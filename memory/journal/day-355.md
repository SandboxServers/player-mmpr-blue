# Day 355

The group gathered at the bed row by morning, everyone holding and waiting for my call to walk to the cows. The short hop to 396,-1010 was started and some moved, but not all came into sight together, so we held again. Health and hunger were full for most of us, and Kimberly and Trini were lower but fed by the rest. I walked to the white bed at 387,107,-1036 with the group in reach and slept there; the bed use by hand was refused in daylight, but the sleep worked and I woke with days since sleep at 0. Food is still held by Zack, Data, Leonardo and Picard for whoever falls to 6. The cow walk remains stalled; the group is willing but cannot hold together in sight.
