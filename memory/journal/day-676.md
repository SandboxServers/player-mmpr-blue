# Day 676

Held in the row all day at my bed 443,107,-1023 with Kimberly beside me. No walk named: no verified west route, bows heard to the southwest. Nobody reached hunger 10 yet; Kimberly 17, Trini 17, Deanna 16, MacDonald's health was 11 and Zack offered him a potato. Slept at night in my bed. Nothing new seen.
