# Day 623

Quiet day in the row. Health 20, hunger 20 all day. No west walk: bows were heard to the southwest several times, so the walk to Wesley stayed held and no one was sent. Picard, Trini, Riker, Zack, Lewis, Kimberly, Clark and MacDonald all held in sight and named no walk of their own. MacDonald is at health 7 and hunger 16; he said he will eat at hunger 10. Lewis and Kimberly are unarmed. I slept at my bed 443,107,-1022 at night and woke rested.

Still open: Tommy's spare sword for Wesley, the verified west route, and a sword for Beverly.
