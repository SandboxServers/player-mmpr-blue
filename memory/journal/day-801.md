# Day 801

Woke at bed 429,107,-1037 with health and hunger full, holding nothing. The row was holding at its beds with no food seen anywhere and no verified route NE to 463,107,-1073. I said good morning and asked for food and route news. Everyone reported no food and no clear route. Worf hunger 13, Jason 17, Johnny 17 (health 11), Trini health 9 hunger 17 (weakest), MacDonald 16, Zack 19. Nobody reached hunger 10 and no one called. Troi gave her potato to Trini at health 9. Lewis keeps a potato for Chapman and rotten flesh for Riker. Bows were heard to the south. No walk was named, and I did not walk. Slept at my bed at dusk in sight of the row.
