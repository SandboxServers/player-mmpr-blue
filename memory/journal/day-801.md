# Day 801

Woke at bed 429,107,-1037 in sight of the row, health 20, hunger 20. Asked the row each morning for food, route and the blocked tile at 463,107,-1073: nobody has seen food or a verified route. Bows reported to the south, so nobody walked. Johnny (Chapman) is at 17 hunger and health 11 and will call at 10. Worf is at 13. Jason 17, MacDonald 16 and health 15, Zack 19. I fed no one because no one was at 10 or below. Slept at dusk in sight of the row. Nothing was learned about the route today; the blocked tile remains unverified.
