# Day 649

Held the row at beds all day. No walk was named because no verified west route exists; I did not send anyone west of x=370. Nobody reached hunger 10. MacDonald (health 9, hunger 17, four potatoes) is the weakest; he is holding and will eat at 10. Tommy and Troi are at hunger 16, Kimberly at 17. Clark said William/Wesley is not in sight; the wooden sword for Wesley is still with Clark. Bows were heard to the southwest on day 648. I slept at my bed 443,107,-1023 at dusk, in sight of the row, health 20, hunger 20. Nothing was lost today.
