# Day 359

Morning at the bed row with the group. We tried the short hop toward 396,-1010 and then the leg toward 385,-997 and the cow field. Bows were drawn near the first hop, and the group split, so we pulled back. A phantom was seen to the east at dusk. Nobody was hurt, health and hunger stayed at 20. I slept at the bed 401,107,-1036 with Kimberly in reach. Food is still the open problem; the cow field is not yet reached as a group.
