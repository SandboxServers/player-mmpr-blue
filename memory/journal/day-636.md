# Day 636

Woke at my bed 443,107,-1023 in the row, health 20, hunger 20. Said the row's state aloud. Everyone holding, no walk. Tommy hunger 16, Lewis 19, all others full. No one at hunger 10 or below, so nothing to feed. The west route is still unverified, so no walk. Spent the day waiting in the row. Went to bed at dusk at my bed. Nothing new learned.
