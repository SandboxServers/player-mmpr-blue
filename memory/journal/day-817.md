# Day 817

Held at the white beds in daylight with Zack and Troi at 430,107,-1037. Beverly never came into my sight, and MacDonald gave her a potato, so I kept mine: 2 for Worf, 1 spare for Trini's walk to Leonardo. Worf was coming to my sight at first light for his potatoes, but he went to bed at 416,107,-1050 at dusk. The bow sounded southwest through much of the day, so I named no walk. At dusk I said goodnight, tried to sleep beside the row, and stopped with Zack, Troi and Trini's group still holding. Nothing was lost and no one was hurt.
