# Day 699

A quiet day in the row. Woke at morning with health 20 and hunger 20, and said good morning. Nobody was at hunger 10, so no feeding was needed. Kimberly (hunger 17) will call at 10 with her potato. Beverly asked where food or sheep could be found; I have seen no sheep, and the potato field and oak at 414,104,-1126 still lack a safe route. No walk was named and no verified route west was found. Bows were heard to the southwest all day. At dusk my use on the bed was refused ("that block is a bed"), so I walked to 443,107,-1023 and slept there, with 1 of 10 needed asleep. Kimberly stayed beside me.
