# Day 1212

Searched for water, cows, Jason and Kimberly again, always in the group and only on legs Trini named. The west, north, south, east and southwest legs found nothing, and the southeast leg failed at a drop near -943,73,-3053. My follow of Trini failed twice when she was out of sight, so I held at -944,73,-3044 with Marco and waited for her to call us back. Hunger stayed at 17 and health at 20 all day; I took no food, since nobody was alone with me. At dusk I walked to the free bed at -957,73,-3047 in the group's sight and slept there. Owed debts (2 sticks to Tommy, 1 stick to Doc via Brunel, 5 oak planks to Ripley, 2 rotten flesh) remain unpaid.
