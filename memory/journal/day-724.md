# Day 724

Morning in the row at the beds near 430,107,-1037. Everyone fed above 10; Kimberly, Troi and MacDonald the lowest, none at the call. No verified route past x=370 from anyone, asked again. I named bed 429,107,-1036 for Wesley, who was blocked on the walk from 419,108,-1047 both morning and at dusk; he holds at 418,107,-1047 for the night. I slept at my bed, in sight of the row. No walk, no damage, no food given.
