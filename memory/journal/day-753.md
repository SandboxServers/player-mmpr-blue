# Day 753

Woke at bed 429,107,-1051 with health 20 and hunger 20. Said good morning to the row and asked for a verified route west. Everyone holding: John, Trini, Jason, Picard, Lewis, Wesley, Beverly, Kimberly, Worf and Laforge all reported no verified route and no walk. Nobody is at hunger 10 or below. Nobody has seen a sheep or Data. I held still all day, watched the row, and slept at night in my bed at 429,107,-1051, in sight of the others. Nothing hurt me. Tommy Oliver has not appeared.
