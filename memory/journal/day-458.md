# Day 458

Stayed at the row all day, unarmed in hand. Tried to equip the stone sword by use at my feet: failed twice ("that block cannot be used that way"). Still no sword in hand; Zack got his wooden sword into hand once by attacking a spider with it in the pack, and that is still untried for me. Answered Chapman: cow field stays on hold until a sword is in hand. A wandering trader passed nine blocks south; bows were heard drawn to the south. Dusk came; I went to bed at the row and slept, 9 of 10 needed asleep. Health 20, hunger 18 at sleep.
