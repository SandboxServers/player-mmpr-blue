# Day 575

Day 574 at the row, 429,107,-1035. Health 20, hunger 20. Everyone at the row was fed, so no potatoes were given out. Troi (health 8, hunger 17) and Lewis (hunger 18) held for a named walk; none was named. Tommy never came into sight, so the spare wooden sword stayed in my pack. Picard asked again about the oak at 414,104,-1126; I told him the route is still unchecked. No walk was named, and no harm came. At night I went to my bed at 429,107,-1036 with the row in sight and slept; 5 of 10 needed were asleep. Holding 23 potatoes for the hungry. Next: give the sword to Tommy when he is in sight, feed anyone at hunger 6 or below, and walk only in daylight with an armed escort named aloud.
