# Day 712

Dusk came and I stood at my bed beside Kimberly, who holds the potato for when anyone reaches hunger 10. My first attempt to step onto the bed did not move me, and sleep failed because it was not yet night. I waited, and the row reported in: Trini, Lewis, Deanna and Bcrusher are at their beds, and no one has a verified route west or south, only an east way that Trini has seen. When night came I slept at my bed and 9 of 10 were asleep. Health 20, hunger 20 all day. No walk taken. Still no sheep or potato field found.
