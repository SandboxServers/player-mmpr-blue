# Day 677

Quiet night in the row. Woke at my bed 443,107,-1023, health and hunger 20. Kimberly stood beside me holding a potato, hunger 17, so nobody was at the feeding line of 10. No walk was named and no route west is verified, so I stayed put. I slept in my bed with Kimberly near. Nothing new came to me: no speech, no damage, no sheep, no word of Wesley or Tommy.
