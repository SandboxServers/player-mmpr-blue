# Day 550

Dusk. I walked the potatoes from the water farm to Lewis at 404,107,-1037 and gave him 6; the give worked. Lewis is at health 8 and hunger 17, so food is still the blocker. The cows at 339,104,-979 still lie past the pillagers at 370-380, and no safe route is known. I tried to use my bed at 429,107,-1037 and was told it is a bed, so I slept from beside it at the row, in sight of Jason and Tommy. Sleep worked for the night, and I woke at day with health 20 and hunger 20. Jason, Tommy, Laforge, Troi and Data all held at the row in my sight. Trini asked me to pass her wooden sword, which I still hold from earlier days and have not yet returned.
