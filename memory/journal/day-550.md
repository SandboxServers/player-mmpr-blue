# Day 550

Held at the row all day in sight of the others. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast almost the whole morning, so no walk west was made, and the route to the cows at 339,104,-979 is still unknown. I gave 12 potatoes to Clark for Lewis and Riker (worked), and I gave 6 potatoes to Jason for Lewis (his own reply: Lewis first). Data's give at the chest failed because he did not pick up, so the potatoes went to Jason instead. I slept in the bed at 429,107,-1037 in the row's sight at dusk. Tommy, Trini, Troi, MacDonald and Data all reported holding at the row. Still no safe route to food.
