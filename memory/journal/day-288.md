# Day 288 (covers days 268 to 288)

Food was the whole story. From day 268 to 288 I found no food except two beef from one cow at the west lake near -288,67,258 on day 288. I died to phantoms at the white beds on the night of day 267 and again at dawn on day 284, both times with nothing in my pack; I woke at spawn both times, and on day 284 Macdonald returned my wooden sword. Sleep at the beds was refused on most nights, blocked by crowding and creepers, and it worked only beside the group. A scout east on day 282 found only deep water and stone; I climbed a stone pillar at -190,57,298 to get out of the water and walked back. The pig lead at -336,64,229 was empty on day 268. On day 288 I gave one beef to Trini as promised, but two gives meant for Troi landed on Data; the same mistake happened with wheat seeds on day 282, which went to Lewis instead of Clark. Lessons: check the name and reach at the moment of each give, stay beside the group before dusk, and do not expect a wooden sword to stop phantoms.
