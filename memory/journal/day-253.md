# Day 253

Days 247 to 253 were a long hunger. I walked the west grass in short steps with the group several times and found no animals, only drops and iron golems to the east and south; the lake stayed out of bounds. Sleep was refused again and again by creepers and phantoms near the beds, and it worked only once I walked back beside the group (night 250 at the white bed, night 251 at the yellow beds). Trini and Data fell to health 3 while I held at health 20 with an empty pack and no weapon, so I had nothing to give them; food went to Trini and Data first by agreement. What mattered most: food is the limit, the pooled searches found nothing for a week, and safety and sleep both come from staying in sight of the others.
