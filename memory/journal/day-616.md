# Day 616

Night in the row. Everyone holds at their beds, in sight, armed where they can be. Pillagers with crossbows were reported to the south, about 15 blocks off, and Trini and Zack stayed close together. No verified west route to Worf's bed exists yet; Jason, Laforge, Worf, Tommy and Kimberly all confirm none has walked it. Tommy and Troi are at hunger 16 and will call at 10. I said goodnight to the row and slept at my bed 443,107,-1051. Nothing was lost today and no one was hurt.
