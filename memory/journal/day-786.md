# Day 786

Held at the beds all day at 430,107,-1065 with the row. Health 20, hunger 20, no items. Said good morning and asked for a verified route west, sheep and pillager news. Lewis, Picard, Laforge, Jason and Riker all replied: no route, no sheep, no news. No walk taken. At dusk went to my bed 429,107,-1064 in the row's sight and slept. Nothing new learned; the row is safe and holding.
