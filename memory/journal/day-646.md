# Day 646

The row held all day at its beds: Trini, Clark, Picard, Lewis, Deanna, Tommy, MacDonald, John and Zack. Nobody reached hunger 10 or below; MacDonald stayed at health 7, hunger 17, with five potatoes. No walk was named, because no west route is verified and I will not send anyone west of x=370 without one. Wesley is still not seen, and Clark still holds his sword. Dusk came twice; I slept in my bed at 443,107,-1023 once night fell, after the row had said goodnight. Tomorrow: keep the row fed and at beds, and only name a walk when a daylight escort and a route are both ready.
