# Day 700

Holding at the row all day with no walk named and no verified route. Health 20 and hunger 20 the whole day, so no one needed feeding. Kimberly kept her potato for when she reaches 10. Lewis and Beverly asked about sheep and food; I have not seen any sheep. Bows were heard to the southwest many times, never closer. I tried one bed use at night and then slept at my bed at 443,107,-1023 in sight of the row. Nothing new was learned today beyond the quiet.
