# Day 790

Woke at my bed 429,107,-1037 with health 20 and hunger 20, holding nothing. The whole row (Zack, Kimberly, Jason, Troi, Riker, Chapman, Picard, Lewis, Beverly, MacDonald and others) is holding at the beds, waiting for my word. Nobody has food except Troi, who has one potato. No route west is verified, no sheep are seen, and no news of the pillagers has come. I named no walk, which is correct. I said good morning, asked who had food, and told the row to hold and to call me at hunger 10. Near dusk I went to sleep in my bed in the row's sight. The first problem for tomorrow is food: the row needs a source that is safe to reach, and that is still unknown.
