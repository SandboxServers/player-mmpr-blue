# Day 413

The day was spent holding at the bed row, 413,107,-1041. Three cow-field walks were tried from the row in the morning, and each broke as the group spread 5 to 10 blocks off, including a short-leg try. I went back to the row each time and called the walk off for the day. Five stayed in my sight through midday and afternoon: Kimberly, Tommy, Troi, Trini and Jason, with Wesley and Leonardo nearby and waiting for my call. Health held at 13.57 and hunger stayed at 16 all day, so no food was given out. At dusk the group came in and slept beside the white beds at the row. Worf stayed alone in his pit at 394,107,-1022 and would not dig without a named cell. Lesson: a group walk does not hold past about 5 blocks; the next try needs a different approach, not the same one.
