# Day 536

Quiet day at the bed row. Health held steady at 18.8, but hunger stayed at 17, so health does not regenerate. Nobody in the group has food. Lewis (health 7) and Worf are holding at the row in my sight, unarmed or waiting. No west walk. At night I went to my bed at 429,107,-1037; the first sleep was partial (9 of 10), the second worked. I keep two rotten flesh for when hunger falls to 6 or below.
