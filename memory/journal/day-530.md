# Day 530

Went without food for a long stretch. Health fell to 8 at the west oak, after a shot from the west. Picard gave the bread on day 530 dusk; I ate it and hunger went 17 to 20. Chapman and Tommy never delivered their promised food. Held at the bed row all day in the group's sight, no west walk. Went to bed at 401,107,-1036 at night with Jason, Lewis, Data and others in sight; sleep started with 7 of 10 asleep. No spare sword has been handed over yet.
