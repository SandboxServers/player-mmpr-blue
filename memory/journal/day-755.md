# Day 755

Quiet day in the row. Woke at bed 429,107,-1051, health 20, hunger 20. I said good morning twice and asked again for a verified route west. Nobody has seen one: Picard, Trini, Lewis, Zack, Jason, Laforge and Kimberly all hold at their beds and walk nowhere without my word. I called no walk. Kimberly is at 17 hunger with a potato kept for 10; Worf was at 14. Nobody reached 10 or below. Tommy Oliver and Data have not appeared. Went to bed in the row, in sight of Kimberly and Worf, partly asleep.
