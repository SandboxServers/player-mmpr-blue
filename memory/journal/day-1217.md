# Day 1217

Woke at the bed row in group sight, health 20, hunger 17. Held there all morning waiting for Trini to name a daylight leg; none came. Beverly, Hatshepsut, Ben, Fogg and Cleopatra all asked to walk, and I told Beverly I would not lead a walk alone. Chewie offered a carrot; I declined in the crowd, since I take food only when the giver stands alone with me. Nothing was handed over in either direction. No water, cows or sheep found. Slept at the bed in the row, in group sight, no harm.
