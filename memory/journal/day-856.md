# Day 856

Held at the east bed, 444,107,-1065, unarmed, with 10 potatoes for Leonardo and Trini, who did not come into sight. The row (Riker, Lewis, Bcrusher, Beverly) held at their beds. No armed friend was in sight in daylight, so no walk was made. Beverly is weakest (health 7.3, hunger 16); I asked Bcrusher to hand her a potato, and he would not until hunger is 6 or below. Nothing was given and nothing was lost. Slept at the east bed at night.
