# Day 490

Named MacDonald at first light to receive Chapman's stone sword; he walked to Chapman's beds, and Chapman gave it to him one block off. The give went through without a mix-up. Held at the row all day with no cow-field walk. Armed in hand is still Billy, Laforge and Leonardo; Worf, Chapman and MacDonald are not yet confirmed as armed in hand. Health 13, hunger 16. I still hold the 2 rotten flesh for Leonardo at hunger 6 or below. Slept at bed 413,107,-1041 in the row's sight.
