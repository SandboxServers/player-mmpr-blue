# Day 791

Woke at bed 430,107,-1037 with health and hunger 20. The row held at the beds all morning. Nobody has food but Troi's one potato. No sheep, no verified route west, no pillager news. Worf 14, MacDonald 16, Trini 17, Troi 17 are the hungry ones. I called for food and asked that anyone who sees food or a sheep call it out, feeding at hunger 10 first. Nobody walks until a route is verified. At dusk I slept at my bed in the row's sight; only 1 of 10 were asleep at the time of this note.
