# Day 688

Held in the row all day at my bed 443,107,-1023 with no walk; no verified route yet, so no one went out. Health 20, hunger 20 all day. Zack, MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16, three potatoes), Beverly, Trini and Lewis all held in sight and reported in. Told the row: eat at hunger 10, call me if health falls, no walk until a route is verified. At dusk I went to sleep in my bed in sight of the row. Nothing new found; the open questions are still the west route, the sheep, and Wesley.
