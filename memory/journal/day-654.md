# Day 654

A quiet day at the row. I stayed at my bed 443,107,-1023 with health and hunger at 20. Everyone in the row held at their beds in sight and waited for a named walk. None was called, because we still have no verified west route, and the bows heard to the southwest keep us from going west of x=370. I gave Kimberly nothing yet: she was out of my sight, so the potato stayed in my pack and she will call at hunger 10. Nobody reached hunger 10 or below. I slept at my bed at dusk with the row in sight.
