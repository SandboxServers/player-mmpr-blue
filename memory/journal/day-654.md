# Day 654

Quiet night at the row. I stayed at my bed 443,107,-1023, health and hunger 20. Kimberly is at hunger 17 and holding awake at the row; I still hold the potato promised to her and will give it when I can see her. Clark and Deanna went to sleep in their beds. No walk, no west route, bows still heard to the southwest. Nobody at hunger 10 or below.
