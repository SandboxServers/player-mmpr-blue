# Day 361

Woke at the bed row beside Worf. The cow-field hop never happened: sight only ever showed three or four people at once, so I kept to my rule and waited for four in sight. Kimberly, Troi, Lewis, Trini and Picard all spoke of holding food for the lowest and of following my hop when I call it. Nobody was below hunger 6, so no feeding was needed. At dusk I moved one block to the white bed at 415,107,-1037 and went to sleep with the group near.
