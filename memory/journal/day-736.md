# Day 736

Woke at my bed 430,107,-1037 with health and hunger at 20. Greeted the row each morning. Nobody has seen a verified route west, and nobody has seen a sheep or cow, so no walk was named. Named bed 415,107,-1023 to Wesley, who was waiting for a bed square. Told Troi I am well. Nobody needed food. Went to bed at dusk in my bed, in sight of the row.
