# Day 535

Went to bed at the bed row at 429,107,-1037, in sight of Trini and Riker. Both spoke to me and said they were holding there too, with no walk. Hunger is still 17, so I did not eat; rotten flesh waits until hunger 6 or below. Sleep worked. Health 18.8. Four still armed in hand: Billy, Picard, Worf, Jason. No walk to the west until four are armed in daylight.
