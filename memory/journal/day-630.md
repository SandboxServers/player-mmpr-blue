Day 630. A quiet day in the row. I woke at my bed, said the row was holding, and named no walk, since the west route is still unverified and bows were heard southwest. Everyone held in my sight. MacDonald (health 7, hunger 16) and Troi (hunger 16) were fed by others with potatoes and I kept watch; nobody reached hunger 10 that I saw. Nothing was gained or lost. I slept at my bed in the row at night, health 20, hunger 20.
