# Day 323

Searched south with Tommy, Trini and Kimberly; a bow was heard, so we pulled back to the beds. Kimberly was fed from Jason's melon and Billy's chicken went to her. Leonardo (hunger 10) is next for food; Johnny not seen. I still hold one chicken for the weakest. Walked to the white bed at 443,107,-1050 at night and tried to sleep; the sleep was only partly done. Hunger 17, health 20.
