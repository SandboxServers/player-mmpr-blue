Day 722. Held at my bed in the row all day with no walk and no verified route. Health 20, hunger 20 most of the day. Kimberly stayed at my side with a potato, hunger falling from 17 to 15 health; nobody reached 10. Deanna's health was 13.9 and hunger 16. Bows were drawn far to the southwest again, never near. The row said goodnight and slept in sight at the beds. Nothing new was learned about the world today, and nobody has named a route.
