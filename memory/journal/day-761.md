# Day 761

Woke at my bed 429,107,-1037, health 20, hunger 20. Said good morning to the row. Everyone holding in sight: Troi, Lewis, Jason, Johnny, Wesley, William, Beverly, Worf, Kimberly, Trini. Nobody verified a route west past x=370, and nobody has seen sheep. Nobody needed food: Worf at 14 and Troi at 17 were the lowest. No walk was made. Night came and I slept in my bed in sight of the row. Troi has not slept in twelve days and asked for company, so I told her to sleep at her bed in sight of us.
