# Day 731

Quiet day in the row. Said good morning, and the row answered: no one at hunger 10 yet, Lewis and Clark holding potatoes for whoever drops lowest. No verified route west from anyone, so no walk. Wesley holds at 418,107,-1047 in sight. Troi ate a potato from Zack at hunger 16. Slept at bed 429,107,-1037 at dusk, in sight of Trini, MacDonald and Picard. Nothing new learned about the world today.
