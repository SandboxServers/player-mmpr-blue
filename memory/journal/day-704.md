Day 704. Held in the row all day at my bed, 443,107,-1023, with Kimberly beside me holding the potato for hunger 10. Health 20, hunger 20 for me. No walk named. I asked the row who has seen a safe route, sheep or a potato field. Trini, Beverly, Kimberly and Lewis each answered: none seen; only cows known at 434,106,-1113 and 339,104,-979. Bows kept drawing to the southwest all day. Slept at night in my bed in the row, sight of Trini and Kimberly.
