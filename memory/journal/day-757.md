# Day 757

Woke at bed 430,107,-1037 with health 20 and hunger 20. The row held all day at their beds: Jason, Beverly, Troi, Kimberly, MacDonald, Trini, William, Worf and Wesley all reported no verified route west, so nobody walked. Nobody fell to hunger 10 or below, so no food was handed out. Troi (13.9 health, hunger 17) and Kimberly (15, 17) are the ones to watch. Data has still not been seen, and Tommy Oliver has not appeared. I slept in my bed at night in sight of the row.
