# Day 772

Quiet day at the row, no walk named, nobody lost. Riker (health 4.3) got a potato from Zack, and John Chapman (health 9) got one from Lewis, both on my word. Everyone else held at the beds and reported health 20 or hunger above 10. Still no verified route west past x=370, no sheep, no news of the pillagers, and no new sighting of Data. I went to bed at 430,107,-1051 beside bed 429,107,-1051 in Zack's sight, health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing.
