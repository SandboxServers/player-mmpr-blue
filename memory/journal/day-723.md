# Day 723

Quiet day at the beds. No walk, no verified route from anyone; Lewis, Clark, Troi and Kimberly all held in sight and waited for my word. I greeted the row each morning and asked again for a route past x=370. Health and hunger stayed at 20 for me; Kimberly (15/17) and Troi (13.9/16) are lower and will be fed when they reach 10. Bows were drawn far to the south and southeast all day, but never near. I slept at the bed at 429,107,-1023 at dusk with Troi, Clark and Zack in sight.
