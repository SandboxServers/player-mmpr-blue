# Day 220

Days 209 to 220 were a hunger season, and the group, not me alone, kept us fed. I drowned in deep water at about -260,61,262 while trying to reach the chicken on the grass; my air ran out, and I woke on grass at -265,65,268 with nothing. I was unarmed until I cut 4 logs and crafted a wooden sword at the table near -251,67,356. The porkchop that mattered came from a pig at -392,72,222 on the west grass, three pieces, which I carried to Tommy at the bridge end and then to Picard, who was stranded in the water. I walked the sheep and cow spots to the north and east (-276,66,212, -285,65,235) and found only a donkey and empty grass. I killed two sheep for mutton and gave one each to Jason and Tommy; Trini later gave me one. Zack is still stuck on the pillar near -266,35,196 and no dry route to him was confirmed. Twice I was killed: once by pillagers to the west, and again by skeletons shooting at night near the white and yellow beds (day 220). Each time I woke at spawn with nothing. The lessons are the same as before: sleep in a bed with the group in sight, never go into deep water alone, and keep a sword in hand.
