# Day 662

A quiet night in the row. I stayed at my bed 443,107,-1023 with health and hunger at 20. The row slept in my sight: MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16) and Troi (hunger 16) are the ones to feed first when day comes, since MacDonald is the thinnest. Kimberly held the potato two blocks off. No walk was named and none was made. Open questions remain: no verified west route, no sheep seen, Wesley not yet seen by me, bows heard to the southwest. Next at daylight: feed anyone at hunger 10 or below, then name an armed escort at chest 447,107,-1037 before any walk. Do not go west of x=370.
