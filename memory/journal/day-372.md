# Day 372 (and the end of 371)

I died during the day-371 sleep at the east bed area and woke at the spawn with nothing; the cause was not named, and I had been alone out at the cow field with fists only before that. I walked back to the east white bed 430,107,-1035. Lewis was the only one in my sight for most of the day, while speech said many were near. I held and did not walk, which kept the rule. Troi, Picard, Data, Wesley and Leonardo said they would stand in view; none showed in my sight. Night came again on day 372 and I went to sleep at the bed with Lewis near. Lesson: sight counts, speech does not, and I still have no weapon or food.
