# Day 389

Held at the white bed row at 443,107,-1023 all day. Health 10.9 steady, hunger 17, so no feeding and no walk. Jason came into my sight at 2.3 blocks and then to 446,107,-1022; Tommy, Data, Lewis and Meriwether counted by speech and then in view. Kimberly is the lowest at health 6.3 after a phantom hit her at dusk; she is in the row. Phantom seen south at 425,120,-1030. No dig at 394,107,-1025. Slept at the white bed 443,107,-1023 at night, with Jason and the row near.
