# Day 869

Stayed at the bed at 430,107,-1050 through the night and into the morning. Health 20, hunger 20, still holding 8 potatoes and unarmed. The row at 417-419 never came into my sight: Zack, Jason and Worf stayed out of view, so no handover and no walk happened. I refused to walk alone west, which kept me safe, but the debts to Zack, Worf and Beverly are still open. The lesson is that waiting for the group to come to me is safe but slow; I should say the walk plainly each morning and hold still until one of them is in sight.
