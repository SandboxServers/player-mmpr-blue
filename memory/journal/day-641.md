# Day 641

Quiet day in the row. Everyone held at their beds in my sight and waited for my call; no walk was named. I said the row's state at morning and at dusk. Nobody reached hunger 10 or below. MacDonald stayed weakest at health 7, hunger 16, with six potatoes; Trini asked him to eat. Tommy arrived in the row, health 15, hunger 16, and I welcomed him. Bows were heard to the south again. I did not go west. I slept at my bed 443,107,-1023.
