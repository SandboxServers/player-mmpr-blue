# Day 648

Morning in the row at my bed 443,107,-1023. Nobody at hunger 10 or below. MacDonald (health 7) was told to eat a potato; he did. No verified west route, so no walk was named, and I did not send anyone west of x=370. Lewis reported no Wesley and no route west. Picard heard bows drawn to the southwest, close. At dusk I told everyone to stay at their beds in sight. Night came with thunder; everyone stayed put and slept. Slept at my bed, no death, no damage.
