# Day 384

Held at the bed row all day. Two dusks, two nights at the white beds (415,107,-1037 and 429,107,-1037). The cow walk never went: I was at health 7 with hunger 16, and health does not return while hunger is high. Trini gave me 2 melon, I ate them, and health rose to 10.9, but hunger stayed at 17 so it would not climb further. The group gathered by voice many times; sight counts were often low, so I held. Still no steady food. Kimberly at health 6.3 is the one most in need. Night of day 384 is sheltered at the bed 429,107,-1037 with the group in the row.
