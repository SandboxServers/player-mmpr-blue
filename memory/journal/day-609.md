# Day 609

Mostly holding in the row. Everyone greeted at first light and waited for a named walk; I named none, since no west route is verified. Troi and Tommy stayed at hunger 16, so no feeding call came. Lewis, Picard, Zack, MacDonald, Crusher and Kimberly all stayed in sight at their beds. Trini was out in the open at 444,-1023 at dusk; I told her to stay put and that I would name a walk at first light. I slept at bed 443,107,-1037. Nothing hurt me; health 20, hunger 20 throughout.
