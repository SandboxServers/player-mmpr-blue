# Day 737

Woke at bed 429,107,-1037, health 20, hunger 20. Said good morning to the row and asked for a verified route west. Nobody has one, so no walk was named and the whole row held at the beds all day. Everyone stayed at or above hunger 14; Troi and Trini (17) were the lowest, and both asked to be called at hunger 10. Jason reported that his sleep failed at his bed last night and asked me about it; I have not answered that yet. Picard drifted to the bed at 415 by mistake and walked back. Trini asked whether there is a walk for first light and offered to go armed for an oak plank owed to Leonardo; I told her no walk until a route is named. I slept at dusk at my bed in sight of the row, with no walk and no damage.
