# Day 737

Morning: I said good morning to the row and asked for a verified route west. Lewis, Wesley, Worf, MacDonald, Zack, Trini, Troi, Jason and Picard all hold at their beds. Nobody has seen a route west, so no walk was named. Hunger stayed above 10 for everyone (lowest Worf and MacDonald at 16). Nothing was hurt and nothing was lost. At dusk I walked to bed 429,107,-1037, said goodnight, and slept in sight of the row. Sleep is in progress with 1 of 10 needed asleep.
