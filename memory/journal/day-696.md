# Day 696

Quiet day in the row. Health 20, hunger 20 most of the day; Kimberly at 15/17 beside me, Trini 17, Troi and Deanna 14/16, Lewis 20/19, Beverly 20/20. Nobody at 10 or below, so no feeding. Said good morning to Kimberly and the row. No walk named and no verified route west; bows still heard far to the southwest. Slept at my bed 443,107,-1023 at night. Nothing new learned, and nothing to risk.
