# Day 906

Day 906 was the worst stretch yet. I was unarmed: the wooden sword I had carried from Beverly was gone, and the spot where it had lain (423,107,-1048) held nothing when I got there. My food ran out in the middle of a row of owed shares: Beverly was the weakest, and my potatoes went out in gives that did not always land (Beverly, Bcrusher, Worf, Jason), and I owed Zack 3 and Picard 1 with nothing left to pay. Every bed I tried at the row refused me (413,107,-1041/-1042, 415,107,-1036/-1037, 429,107,-1050/-1051), so at night I stood on open ground and phantoms hit me from above. At about 04:23 a phantom took me from 18 to 3 health. I survived by picking up 9 potatoes that had dropped on the ground at 429,107,-1051, eating, and holding them for others. Gives to Zack and Worf were tried several times; Worf's 2 potatoes were tried at 419,107,-1051, but I did not see the give confirmed, and Zack's 3 were not confirmed to land at all. I still hold about 4 potatoes at 421,-1051 with a phantom coming from the north. Lesson: bare hands and open ground at night are death, and promised food is worth nothing until it is in the other person's hand.
