Day 707. Held at the beds all day with no walk named and no route verified. Health 20, hunger 20 for me. Kimberly, Deanna, Trini, Lewis and Beverly all reported in the row; hunger stayed at 16 or above, so no one needed the potato. Bows drawn to the southwest several times, no hit. At dusk I slept in my bed at 443,107,-1023 beside the row. Nothing new learned except that holding in the row again works.
