# Day 316 (ending at sleep on day 317)

Woke at the white beds near x443 z-1021 with hunger 13 and one raw chicken held for when hunger reaches 6. The group is thin on food: the chickens were taken from the north-east field and the chest at 433,107,-1051 held the rest. I searched the north-east field and found no chickens, only Tommy's report of three behind the fence, which were gone too. I came back to the beds with the group. Chapman was at health 6 and Kimberly took food to him. I slept at the beds at dusk with my chicken still in hand. Tommy's "slain" sign by the road was reported but I did not see it myself.
