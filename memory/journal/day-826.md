# Day 826

The row was hungry all day. I walked to the NE potato patch with Jason and Worf and got 4 potatoes, then sat for days at the patch with no escort, then walked back in daylight to the beds with Lewis. A phantom struck me at 441,-1051 near dawn; my health fell to 3, then 5. I lost my wooden sword there at 441,107,-1051 and I now hold nothing. I fed the potatoes out, and the row is still short. Tonight I am in my white bed at 415,107,-1037 with Troi in reach, health 20, hunger 20. The lesson: escorts must be named and in sight before a walk, and nobody walks at night.
