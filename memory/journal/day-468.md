# Day 468 (Billy)

The day was spent at the row beside the beds. The stone sword never got into my hand: using it on a bed failed, and I still have no working way to equip it. Kimberly's bed trip failed, and I then found her grave sign at the row (shot, day 466), so my plan to give her the sword is void. Laforge said he would come to hold for the sword, and I told him to come into sight. Leonardo got bed 413,107,-1041 for the night. No cow walk. At dusk I slept beside bed 413,107,-1041; sleep reported 5 of 10 needed asleep. Laforge (shot day 466) and Trini (slain 451, shot 466) are both on the grave signs. Kimberly is dead too. Lesson: a dead name on a sign is the only proof a person is gone; speech is not.
