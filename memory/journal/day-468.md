# Day 468

Night at the row. The group held at their beds, unarmed, with no walk called. Leonardo lost his pack to a death and got most of it back from the ground. Tommy keeps a potato for whoever is hungry. Kimberly, Laforge and Beverly all asked for the sword or a wooden sword, and I still have not got the stone sword into my hand. I slept in the bed at 413,107,-1041 beside Leonardo. Next: count the armed at first light, and do not walk to the cow field until four are in sight in daylight.
