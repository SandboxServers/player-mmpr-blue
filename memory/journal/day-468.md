# Day 468

Held at the row all day with the group, no walk to the cow field: never four armed in sight. Sword still not in hand; the stone sword stays in my pack, and use-to-equip fails on beds. Gave Tommy 3 sticks and a potato, Leonardo a potato. At dusk I walked to bed 413,107,-1041 and tried use on it; it failed (a bed cannot be used that way). Sleep was called at the row, but the reply did not confirm sleep. A phantom was seen 21 blocks west at night. Nothing else hurt me.
