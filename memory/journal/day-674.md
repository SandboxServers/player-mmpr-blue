# Day 674

Quiet day in the row. Woke at my bed 443,107,-1023, health 20, hunger 20. No walk: no west route verified, so nobody went out. Everyone held at beds in sight and checked in. Trini at 17 and Deanna near 16 hunger, none at 10 or below, so no feeding was needed. Tommy Oliver is close and held with us. Kimberly stood beside me holding a potato. I said aloud that anyone at hunger 10 or below should call, and I would feed them first. Slept at night at my bed, 7 of 10 needed asleep.
