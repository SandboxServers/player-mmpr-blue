# Day 686

Held at my bed 443,107,-1023 all day in the row. No walk was named. Health 20, hunger 20. The row (Trini 17, Kimberly 15, Troi 14, Lewis and Zack 19-20, Beverly 20) all held at their beds in my sight. Nobody reached hunger 10, so no food was given. Bows kept sounding far to the southwest, so nobody went that way. Trini asked to walk to the row table for food; I said no walk and that I hold no food. Slept at dusk in my bed (accepted this time). Sound and speech came in many times, and nothing was hurt.
