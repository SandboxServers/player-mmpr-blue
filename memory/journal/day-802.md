Day 802. Night thunder, then a clear day and dusk. I stayed at bed 429,107,-1037 in sight of the row the whole time, health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing. Trini (health 9 to 10) got Troi's potato and ate it, so she is fed for now. No food has been seen by anyone, and the NE route to 463,107,-1073 is still unverified, so nobody walked. Worf is at hunger 13, MacDonald at 16. I named no walk. I slept at dusk in sight of the row.
