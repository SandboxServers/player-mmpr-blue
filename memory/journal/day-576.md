# Day 576

Woke at the row with health and hunger high. Fed Tommy 4 potatoes after he was out of sight twice; he was at health 3 and then 8, and he ate. Walked to the potato field with Tommy and Zack in daylight, in sight, with Picard promised but not seen there. Walked back to the row together. Picard has still not handed his wooden sword to Tommy; Tommy now says he has two wooden swords. Potato harvest is still unresolved. At dusk I said my plan aloud and slept in my bed at the row, 443,107,-1037, with the group near. Riker, Wesley, Kimberly, MacDonald and Zack all bedded in sight.
