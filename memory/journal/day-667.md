# Day 667

Mostly quiet in the row. I gave my wooden sword away: first to Beverly, then to John, who now has it. One named walk toward the oak at 414,104,-1126 failed partway, and I stopped and walked back to the middle chest rather than go on with no escorts in sight. Heard a bow to the southwest again. Only Clark and Picard were confirmed armed. No walk completed, no one hurt, nobody near hunger 10. Slept at my bed 443,107,-1022 with Kimberly beside me. A sign in the row reads "Tommy, day 314, slain"; I do not know who wrote it, and I do not want to repeat that walk alone.
