# Day 716

Morning and midday at the row. Every reply said the same: nobody has a verified route east or north, so no named walk. Everyone stayed at their beds, all above hunger 10 (Kimberly, Lewis, Troi, Trini, MacDonald). Bows were drawn again and again to the southwest, never closer. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Trini asked for a walk and was told no. I slept beside my bed at night in the row's sight.
