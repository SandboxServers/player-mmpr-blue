# Day 1207

Woke at my bed on the row at health 20, hunger 18. The row stayed crowded all day, and no food reached me. Zack's first search for Jason and Kimberly failed; he regrouped at the row, and Trini and Tommy agreed to go with us in daylight. The walk never started, so nothing new was found. By dusk MacGyver was the lowest at health 9.8, and Aragorn and Clark offered him melon. Adam and Indy looked for water, and none has been seen nearby. I slept at my bed in the group's sight, and it took. Nothing is owed to me; I still owe 2 sticks to Tommy, 1 to Doc via Brunel, 5 planks to Ripley, and 2 rotten flesh to the weakest.
