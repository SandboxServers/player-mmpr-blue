# Day 806

Woke in bright daylight at 430,107,-1023 holding 3 potatoes and a wooden sword. Worf never came into sight all day, so the potatoes stayed with me. John, Troi, Zack and MacDonald all held, each saying Worf first; none was below 10 hunger. No walk was named and the NE route stays unverified. Health stayed 20. At dusk I wrote the handoff and slept beside bed 429,107,-1023; sleep worked for 7 of 10.
