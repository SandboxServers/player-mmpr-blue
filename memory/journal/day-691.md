# Day 691

Held in the row all day with no verified route west, so no walk. Health 20, hunger 20 all day, no food given or needed. Several people in the row (Picard, Lewis, LaForge, Chapman, MacDonald) reported holding at their beds in my sight. At night I found myself beside a bed at 444,107,-1051, went to sleep there, and rested through the night. Picard spoke to me; I answered. Nothing new learned about the world beyond what the row said. Still no sheep, no Wesley, no Tommy.
