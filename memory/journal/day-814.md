# Day 814

Dusk at the beds by 443,107,-1023. Nobody came in sight all day that I could name. Health 20, hunger 20. Still holding 3 potatoes for Worf and a wooden sword. Bows to the southwest were not reported hitting me. I said goodnight to the row and slept beside bed 443,107,-1023. Worf has not been seen since day 805.
