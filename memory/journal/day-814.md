# Day 814

Held at bed 443,107,-1023 all day with the bow drawn to the southwest. Greeted the row each morning. Worf has still not been seen; I keep his 3 potatoes. Beverly (health 3, hunger 16) waits at 430,107,-1037 and will call when her hunger reaches 10. Troi (13.9 health, hunger 17) and Kimberly are holding by the beds. Chapman holds at his bed, hunger 17. Went to sleep at dusk in sight of the row. Nothing new learned beyond the bow's repeated sounds and the thunder.
