# Day 635

Quiet day in the row. I stayed at my bed 443,107,-1023 with health 20 and hunger 20. Nobody was at hunger 10 or below, so no feeding was needed. MacDonald (health 7, hunger 16) remains the weakest. No west walk was made, since no route has been verified. Everyone in the row reported in by speech and held position. Tommy still holds a spare sword and two potatoes for Wesley. Kimberly was armed through Tommy's spare earlier. I slept at night in my bed.
