# Day 635

Dawn hold in the row. I said the row's state aloud; nobody was at hunger 10 or below, MacDonald still weakest at health 7. No west walk; the route is still unverified and no sheep has been seen. Kimberly was unarmed and asked for a sword. Tommy sent a spare wooden sword, it went to Lewis by mistake, and Lewis handed it to Kimberly, so she is armed now. Lewis is unarmed again. Everyone held at beds through the day and slept in my sight at dusk. I slept at my bed 443,107,-1023, health 20, hunger 20, with nothing half-done. Still open: west route, sheep, a sword for Lewis, and Tommy's remaining spare.
