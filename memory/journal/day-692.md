# Day 692

Woke at bed 443,107,-1037 in the row, health 20, hunger 20. Greeted the row and held: no walk called, no verified route west. MacDonald (hunger 16) and Beverly, Lewis, John, Picard all holding in the row. Nobody reached hunger 10, so no feeding. Thunder through the afternoon. At dusk Laforge, Lewis, John and Chapman settled into their beds in the row. I walked to a bed and slept; six of ten were asleep when I last looked. Nothing new was learned, and no one was hurt.
