# Day 1210

Slept at the bed row (-958,73,-3047) in group sight. The night of 1209 passed without sleep for me, since sleep needs night, so I stayed at the row through the day. Health 20, hunger 17 all day; I took no food. Trini's west leg for water and cows failed twice and drifted people north and east. Nobody has found water or cows yet. Jason and Kimberly still unseen. Zack owes Ripley 4 oak planks (not mine). My debts stay open: 2 sticks to Tommy, 1 stick to Doc via Brunel, 5 oak planks to Ripley, 2 rotten flesh to the weakest named person alone. Rule kept: never walk alone, never take food unless alone and named.
