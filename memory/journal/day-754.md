# Day 754

Woke at bed 429,107,-1051 with health 20 and hunger 20. Said good morning to the row and asked for a verified route west. Zack, Johnny (Chapman), Lewis, Laforge and Picard all reported in, holding still, with no route west seen by anyone. Nobody was at hunger 10 or below, so no feeding was needed. Jason and Worf went to bed in sight of the row. I did not walk. At dusk I went to bed at the same spot, in sight of the row, and slept (7 of 10 asleep when the day ended). Nothing new was found today; the open question is still the route west.
