# Day 264

Days 254 to 264 were one long food hunt with no meat until day 261. I walked the west grass with the group, scouted the sheep field south (empty), the north-west grass (no animals, only water and drops), and the north (water and drops only). On day 261 I killed a cow on the grass west of the lake near -288,67,257 and got beef; it went to Tommy, not to Worf as I had promised, and the wheat went to Picard for the bread pool. On day 263 phantoms attacked on the west grass and cut my health to 10; I got back to the group and the yellow bed. Sleep was refused again and again near the white beds by golems and creepers, and it worked only when I was beside the group. Health was 10 at the last clear reading, hunger 15 by night 264, and my pack held logs, planks, sticks and a wooden sword but no food. Jason, Trini and Laforge were the weakest and first in line for any meat; nobody had food to give them.
