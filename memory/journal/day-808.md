# Day 808

Woke beside bed 443,107,-1023 at health 20, hunger 20. Worf still not in sight; I keep his 3 potatoes. Zack (19), Troi (17), John (health 11, hunger 17), MacDonald (15, hunger 16), Kimberly (20) all held at the beds with no walk. A bow was drawn again and again to the southwest, so nobody walked. At dusk I slept beside 443,107,-1023 in the row's sight. Nothing was eaten and no one died.
