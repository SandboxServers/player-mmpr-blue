# Day 545

Stayed at my bed at 429,107,-1037 through the day. Hunger 17, health 18.8, two rotten flesh held, eat only at hunger 6. Nobody in the group has food; cows were reported northeast at 339,104,-979 by Chapman, unseen by me. Armed count in sight: Picard, Jason, MacDonald, Clark (wooden swords), four of seven, so I named no walk. Lewis at health 7 is the one to watch. Slept again at dusk with three of ten asleep.
