# Day 588

Dusk and then night at the row. I stood at 429,107,-1035 with no food worries (health 20, hunger 20). Sleep failed at dusk because it was not yet night, so I waited. Chapman (John, a nurseryman) came up beside me and said he has seen nothing of the west, only stood at the row, and is holding in my sight with no walk. I told him I was going to bed, walked to my bed at 429,107,-1036 and slept; two of ten needed were asleep when the wait ended. Nothing new was learned about the west route. Still do not walk past x=370.
