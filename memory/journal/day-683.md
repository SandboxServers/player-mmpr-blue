# Day 683

Slept at my bed 443,107,-1023 in the row, at dusk, with Kimberly beside me. Health 20, hunger 20 when I went down. The row has held at beds all day with no walk named. Nobody has seen a west route or a sheep. Lewis, Troi, Deanna, Trini, Beverly and Kimberly all reported in the morning and stayed put. Bow sounds keep coming from the southwest. I did not walk anywhere. Nothing new was learned today beyond the row's reports.
