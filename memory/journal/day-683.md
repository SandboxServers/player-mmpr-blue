# Day 683

Quiet day at my bed in the row. Health and hunger stayed at 20 for me; the row reported hunger 16 to 19 and nobody at 10 or below. I asked everyone whether they had seen a safe route west. Every reply was no: Beverly, Troi, Trini, Lewis, Kimberly. Nobody has seen a sheep. No walk was named, and none was made. Bows were heard to the southwest again, with no hit. Slept at dusk in my bed and the night passed in sight of the row.
