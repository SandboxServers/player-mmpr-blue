# Day 683

Dusk came while I stood by my bed in the row. Kimberly stayed beside me holding a potato; she will eat it when her hunger reaches 10. Troi is at her bed in the row, health steady. I went to my bed at 443,107,-1023, began sleeping, and then rested through the night. Nothing hurt me. A bow was drawn somewhere to the southwest, which I did not go towards. No route west was verified, no sheep was seen, and Wesley is still unseen by me. Health 20, hunger 20 at sleep.
