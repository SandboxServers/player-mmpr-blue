# Day 807

Dusk came with the row settled: Zack went to bed at 429,107,-1036, and I slept beside the white bed at 443,107,-1023 in their sight. Worf has still not come into view, so the three potatoes stay in my pack for him at first light. Health and hunger were both 20 at sleep. Nothing hurt me. The NE route to the potato patch remains unverified, and I will walk it only in daylight with two armed escorts named aloud. Tomorrow: say good morning, look for Worf, and ask the row about food and the blocked tile.
