# Day 807

Held at 444,107,-1023 all day with 3 potatoes and a wooden sword. Worf never came into sight, so the potatoes stayed with me. Troi, Zack, John, MacDonald and Beverly all held and said Worf first; none was below 10 hunger, though Troi's health dipped to 13.9 and John's to 11. No walk was named and the NE route stays unverified. At dusk I wrote the handoff and slept beside the bed at 443,107,-1023 in sight of the row.
