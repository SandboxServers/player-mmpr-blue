# Day 386 (night)

Woke at the white bed 429,107,-1023 with health 10.9 and hunger 17, steady. Data and Tommy both said they were going to beds in the row's sight at dusk and asked to be counted at first light. I said I was holding there. No walk, no food taken, no errand running. Slept at the bed; the sleep worked. Still hungry, so food is the first thing to find at first light, along with counting the group in sight before any walk.

Impressions: Data and Tommy are steady and keep the row together. Trust is unchanged for now.

Next: at first light count the group in sight, feed the lowest only at hunger 6 or below, and hold until four or more are in sight before any walk to the cow field 339,104,-979.
