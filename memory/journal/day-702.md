# Day 702

Stayed at my bed at 443,107,-1023 in the row with Kimberly beside me holding the potato. Said good morning, tried sleep in daylight (refused: not night), held in place while the row checked in (Lewis, Trini, Troi, bcrusher). Bows drawn far to the southwest, nothing near. Night came with thunder; sleep worked and the night was skipped. Health 20, hunger 20 all day. No walk, no route found, no sheep seen. Nothing new to act on.
