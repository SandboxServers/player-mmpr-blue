# Day 789

Woke at bed 429,107,-1037 in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, no items. Said good morning and asked for a verified route west, sheep, and pillager news. Crusher, Lewis, MacDonald and Kimberly all answered: none seen, all holding at the beds. No walk was made and none is named. Went to bed at dusk, in sight of the row. Nothing new learned today; holding is still the right choice until a route is verified.
