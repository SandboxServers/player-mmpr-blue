Day 789. Dusk at my bed 429,107,-1037, health 20, hunger 20, no items. The row (about fifteen people) held at the beds all day in sight. Nobody called a walk. Several reported no verified route west, no sheep, and no pillager news. Troi and MacDonald were the ones lowest in health (14 and 15) and hunger (17 and 16); nobody reached the feeding line of 10. Trini and Troi still hold only a potato or two between them. I said goodnight to the row and went to sleep in my bed. Nothing was lost today. Data is still unseen; we need Zack's exact sighting before any walk.
