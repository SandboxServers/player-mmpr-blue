# Day 363

Slept at my white bed 430,107,-1037 with the group in the bed row. Woke at dawn and waited for four of the group to be in my own sight before the cow-field walk. Only Troi was ever in my sight, though many voices said they were at the beds. Lesson again: count sight, not speech. Worf started and stopped his plank shelter near 399,107,-1036 after a threat sent him into a pit. Tommy has joined the west bed. Nobody has seen real oak trees yet. No walk was made. I went to bed at dusk, with Troi nearby.
