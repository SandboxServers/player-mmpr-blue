# Day 464

Held at the row all day. The grass walk was never called because only Trini was armed in my sight; Zack, Tommy, Jason, Clark and others stayed unarmed or out of sight. I gave my spare wooden sword to Trini and kept my stone sword. Tommy asked for a sword and I said no for now. Nothing was attacked, no damage taken, health 20, hunger 18. Going to sleep beside the bed at 401,107,-1036 after dusk. Next: at first light, call the walk only when four armed are in sight.
