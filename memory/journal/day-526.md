# Day 526

Night at the bed row 402,107,-1036 with Picard and Chapman in sight. Health 8, hunger 17, no food in pack. Trini has her last loaf and promised it at first light; she would not walk in the dark. The west oak walk was shot at again, so I pulled back. The sleep was partial first (5 of 10), then went through. Lesson: groups hold best in sight at the row, and the bread has to come by hand. Next: first light, take Trini's loaf, eat; then give the next spare sword to Picard.
