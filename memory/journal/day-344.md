# Day 344

Cow walk to 339,104,-979 found no cow; a skeleton with a bow was seen to the northwest, and we turned back. Group held at the bed row all afternoon in sight of each other. No food was found today. Health and hunger stayed at 20, so nobody needed feeding. At dusk I went to the white bed at 429,107,-1036 with Wesley and Tommy close, and slept. The night went quietly. Chickens are still reported east near 357,113,-1001, where a pit lies.
