# Day 1201

Night at the bed row, -958,73,-3047, with Zack, Tommy, Bilbo and many others in sight. Health 20, hunger 18 at dusk. Nothing new was found today: Jason and Kimberly still unseen, food still short, no cows or sheep found to the west. Nobody took or gave food this night. I slept in a bed in the group's sight and woke to no new events. Owed debts unchanged; I give only to a named person alone in reach.
