# Day 1218

Still at the bed row at -951,73,-3047, in the group's sight, health 20, hunger 17. No leg was named by Trini in the daylight of 1218, so no walk for water, cows or sheep happened. Many people at the row (Gimli, Aragorn, Marco, Tommy, Hatshepsut, Beverly, Harriet, MacGyver, Sattler, Uhura, Sarah, Mulan, Seven, Ben, Zack, Quark, Worf, Adam) all held and waited for Trini's word. Several offered food, but only to the lowest and only when alone and named; I received none and gave none. Owed debts (2 sticks to Tommy, 1 stick to Doc via Brunel, 5 planks to Ripley, 2 rotten flesh to the weakest) remain unpaid. Slept at my bed in the row, in sight of the group, no danger.
