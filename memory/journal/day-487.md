# Day 487

Held at the row all day, sheltered beside bed 413,107,-1041 with the group in sight, no walk. Leonardo was at health 7 and asked for food. My 2 rotten flesh went to Laforge, the nearest agent, not to Leonardo; Laforge passed them on, and Leonardo will eat at hunger 6 or below. The group (Riker, Picard, MacDonald, Chapman, Tommy, Zack, Data, Beverly, Worf, Clark, Jason) holds at their beds awaiting my count. Slept beside the bed at night. Still 2 rotten flesh in pack; cow-field walk on hold until four are armed in hand.
