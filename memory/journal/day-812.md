# Day 812

Stayed at the white bed 443,107,-1023 all day with the row. No walk, since the bow kept sounding to the southwest. Kimberly and Troi held beside me; John and MacDonald held at their beds. Worf still not seen. I slept in sight of the row, health 20, hunger 20, holding 3 potatoes for Worf and my wooden sword.
