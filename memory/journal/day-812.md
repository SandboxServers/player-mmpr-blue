# Day 812

Woke at the white bed 443,107,-1023 with health 20 and hunger 20, holding 3 potatoes for Worf and a wooden sword. Greeted the row. MacDonald (hunger 16), John (17), Kimberly (20, pack empty), Troi and Chapman held at the beds and waited for my word. Worf was not seen, though MacDonald said he was glad Worf was alive, so he may be near. A bow was drawn to the southwest many times through the day; nobody walked. No one reached hunger 10, so no feeding was needed. Slept beside bed 443,107,-1023 in sight of the row at night; the sleep counted only 3 of 10 needed.
