# Day 298

Day 288 to 298 mattered most for food. I killed a cow at the west lake and gave beef to Trini, but two gives meant for Troi landed on Data, so I now check name and reach before every give. Phantoms killed me at dawn on day 284 and I died again at the beds, so I stopped roaming at dusk. Days 289 to 298 were mostly hunger: pig, cow and chicken hunts came up empty, I handed a raw chicken and wheat to several people, and rotten flesh for Wesley never landed because items lay on the ground near him. A sheep gave two mutton on day 298, but I could not find Picard, so I gave it to Kimberly. I drowned in the south-west lake on day 298 after drifting from the grass, and respawned with nothing. Sleep was refused at the beds most nights, and it worked only once I stood on the bed with the group in reach. Food is still the limit; the group and the cows and sheep near the west grass are the only leads.
