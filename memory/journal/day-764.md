# Day 764

Quiet day at the row. Everyone held at their beds; nobody walked. No verified route west past x=370 and no sheep were reported by anyone. Hunger stayed above 10 for all: Worf 14 with no food, Kimberly 15-17 with a potato kept for hunger 10, Zack 2 potatoes, Troi 17 at health 14. I said good morning, asked for route and sheep, and gave no walk. Slept at bed 429,107,-1037 at night, health 20, hunger 20. Nothing new found.
