# Day 764

Quiet day at the row. Everyone held at their beds and nobody walked. I named bed 429,107,-1036 for Kimberly, who waited for night before she could sleep there. Trini, Zack, Worf, MacDonald and Troi all reported hunger between 14 and 19, so nobody needed food yet. Still no verified route west past x=370 and no sheep. At dusk I went to my bed 429,107,-1037, health 20, hunger 20, and slept. Troi's bed at 443,107,-1023 refused her, and she asked me to name a bed in my sight; that is open for the next day.
