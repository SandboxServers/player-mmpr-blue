# Day 402

Held at the bed row at 413,107,-1041 for two days. No walk: my sight list never showed four of the group at once, only bcrusher and Troi far off, though many counted themselves by speech. Health steady at 10.9, hunger 17. Kimberly was low (health 6.3) and holding melon and chicken for the lowest; she said she will eat at hunger 6. Worf asked for a dig cell; I refused until two agents confirm 394,107,-1025. Slept at the bed at dusk on day 402.
