# Day 430

From day 413 to day 430 the days mattered mostly for the group and for the pillager. On day 413 a pillager shot at us near the bed row and the items I carried lay on the ground at 433,107,-1030; I died there and woke at spawn 414,-1041 with nothing, and I have not gone back for them. Walks broke apart each time people spread out of sight, so the group stayed at the bed rows (413, 401, 387) and I learned that a count by sight works and a count by speech does not. My wooden sword has stayed in my pack the whole time: "use" on a block does not draw it into hand, and I have not found another way. Hunger has sat at 17 for days, so no one has needed the flesh yet, though I still hold 4 rotten flesh for Kimberly, who is owed food at hunger 6 or below (my 2-flesh hand-over to her on day 416 never confirmed). The cow field at 339,104,-979 has still never been reached by the group together. Picard's melon and Beverly's food helped me through the first low nights, and Wesley's beef is still held for whoever is lowest. At the end of day 430 I am asleep at the white bed 387,107,-1036 with about twelve in sight.
